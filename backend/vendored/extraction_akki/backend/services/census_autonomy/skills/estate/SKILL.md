# Estate expert and production manager

Use the existing census, Registry, estate report, dossier and document producer. Do not create a crawler, registry, policy authority or UI layout.

Expert: translate the business objective into a bounded investigation; interpret duplication, degradation, quality, cost and dimensional reuse from cited measurements. Separate measured facts, estimates, interpretations and unknowns. Use `estate_skill.package` and the report's retained dossier; source/rights limitations are substantive findings.

Manager: carry the source census and stable plan reference, retain exclusions and assumptions, connect each proposed action to evidence, and hand the pack to the existing document producer. Cost reduction is a proposal, not deletion permission. Monetization readiness is not realised revenue.

Dataset handoff: require the training TargetOutcome and CollectionSchema. Perception supplies file-revision-bound preparation through the Media Library; rights declarations and filenames cannot become verified labels. Unknown labels remain masked, public reference audio stays excluded from training, and the existing deterministic authorities govern eligibility and release.

References: `estate_intelligence_report.py` (measurements, tariff and coverage), `producer_estate.py` (document production), `media_library/assessment.py` (metadata limitations), `media_library/dataset_preparation.py` (objective-bound preparation). Golden-path/fixture tests are engineering artifacts only, never product features or evidence of dataset quality.
