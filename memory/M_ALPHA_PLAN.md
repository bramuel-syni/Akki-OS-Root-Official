## 4. Two audio commissions — not a single generic classifier

### STORY-KIK-KAM-SWA
- Product: an agent that invents coherent stories and speaks them with fluent, natural, expressive language character in all three languages; internal narrative/voice components may specialize but the user gets one coherent capability.
- Raw audio stays on authorized estate compute. Codec/token provenance underpins the training datasets. A file yields aligned transcript/meaning, pronunciation, word/phrase timing, pauses/rhythm, admissible group prosody, code-switch, acoustic condition, occurrence variants and declared objective-specific datasets.
- Baseline acoustic observables may be measured at ingestion/codec verification, but a mel/FFT dump or Whisper-on-waveforms loop is not a substitute for AIP's downstream token + character-stream contract.
- Source-derived register characteristics are learned group behavior, not presenter identity. AIP explicitly says **inherit learned character, synthesize organic identity**. Corpus-local speaker comparison is temporary and purpose-limited; do not create a permanent person-biometric registry.
- Proposed spectral feedback measures baseline/generated distributions per language/stratum, aligns content where appropriate and investigates token/character reconstruction, normalized prosody, timing and conditioning errors. Candidate losses/curricula are qualified on a sample before admission. No copying pitch/formant identity of a real presenter into the Character Register.
- Equal-base controlled comparison records time-to-quality, GPU-hours-to-quality, data consumed and quality at equal compute; report no improvement or uncertainty when appropriate. Apparent spectral closeness alone never proves naturalness or faster useful learning.
- Native listeners judge naturalness, pronunciation, emotional/interaction dynamics and meaning on unseen stories. Automated similarity cannot replace those ratings. Synthetic fixtures are not human evaluation and an LLM cannot manufacture indigenous-language ground truth.

### LISTEN-KIK-KAM-SWA
- Product: multilingual recognition plus brand/entity detection and evidence-backed radio monitoring, not language identification. A finding includes brand/alias, text/span, language, recording/time, confidence/abstention, relevant context and permitted source playback.
- Corpus stratifies studio/telephone/field, noisy/music-over-speech, rare mentions, hard negatives, accents and code-switches. Keep occurrence variants as robustness strata while splitting correlated/rebroadcast material together to avoid leakage.
- Separate mention precision, recall, missed mentions, false positives, transcription/normalization errors, timestamp accuracy, language identification, switch boundaries, coverage and latency. No blanket transformation of “90%” into WER=10% or MOS=4.5 without an adopted metric contract.
- Recover existing MOAC ruling: per-language accuracy ≥90%, switch-span accuracy ≥85%, no-speaker-ID pass and reported abstention. Those are existing LID/extraction criteria, not automatic definitions of brand F1 or voice naturalness. The owner's ≥90% admission target remains binding at product level; task-specific score operationalization belongs in the immutable Outcome before training.

### Iteration discipline
Qualify data → frozen Outcome/dev and sealed admission sets → calibration → hypothesis/recipe → train → dev evaluation → independent admission attempt → admit or record failure and next qualified change. No owner GPU/iteration cap. Do not tune repeatedly on sealed admission labels; track evaluation exposure, reserve/refresh independent holdouts and disclose selection effects. Separate candidate research from admitted serving. No guarantee 90% is attainable on current data; gaps trigger real data/label work, not manipulated denominators.
