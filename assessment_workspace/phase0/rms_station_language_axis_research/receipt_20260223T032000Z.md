## Model Corroboration Policy (owner-verbatim HONESTY RULE)

**Owner directive verbatim (4. HONESTY RULE · non-negotiable):**
> whisper-class models do NOT reliably distinguish
> Kikuyu/Luo/Kalenjin/Kamba etc. — where the model can't corroborate,
> the attribution is "station-prior (cited), model-corroboration:
> unavailable" — never present model output as confirming a language
> it can't detect. Where model says Swahili/English it corroborates
> those stations only.

### Whisper-corroboratable languages (per whisper multilingual language-ID coverage)

Confirmed from RMS-portfolio intersection with whisper's reliable set:
- Swahili (whisper: `sw`)
- English (whisper: `en`)

Every other language in the RMS portfolio (Kikuyu, Dholuo/Luo, Kalenjin,
Kamba, Kimeru, Luhya, Ekegusii/Kisii, coastal-Swahili-Mijikenda variants,
Bukusu, Kiembu, Maragoli) is NOT reliably distinguished by whisper-class
models.

### Attribution schema (product-language)

For an asset with station-attribution S and model-detected-language M:
- If station S's primary language is in `{Swahili, English}` AND M ∈ `{Swahili, English}`: attribution = `{station_prior: S.primary_language, model_corroboration: M, corroborated: True}`.
- Otherwise (station S's primary language is vernacular NOT in whisper set): attribution = `{station_prior: S.primary_language, model_corroboration: 'unavailable', corroborated: False}`.
- Attribution NEVER presents model output as confirming a language it can't detect.
