## §A · Discharge summary

CP-M6-1-3 lands `services/audio_plane_v1/channel_condition_profiler.py` — the pluggable-seam mechanism for Audio-Plane §4.3 P067 verbatim:

> §4.3 P067 (V48): "Channel condition — studio / field / telephone band, transmission artefacts, background class — is derived per segment at first ingestion. It is cheap to compute, directly predictive of extraction error, and required for training stratification. It is captured at first ingestion because re-ingesting an estate to recover it is the unaffordable mistake."

Public API:
- `derive_channel_condition_profile(*, segments: Sequence[AudioSegment], backend: ChannelConditionProfilerBackend) -> List[ChannelConditionProfile]`
- `AudioSegment` — **re-exported from CP-M6-1-1** (single source of truth · no refactor of CP-M6-1-1).
- `ChannelConditionProfile` (frozen 5-carrier: `segment_ref` + `channel_condition` + `transmission_artefacts` + `background_class` + `backend_name`).
- `ChannelConditionProfilerBackend` (`Protocol` with `backend_name` attr + `derive(segment_ref, duration_s) -> Mapping[str, Any]` returning the three required keys).
- `UnratifiedChannelConditionInferredBackend` (placeholder; refuses with `[INFERRED-pending-ratification]` marker per D10-family open ruling).
- `CHANNEL_CONDITION_VALUES` = `frozenset({"studio", "field", "telephone_band"})` — §4.3 P067 verbatim three-set (Python-identifier form of the space-separated "telephone band").
- Refusals: `SegmentsEmptyError` (re-exported) · `SegmentInvalidError` (re-exported) · `BackendUnratifiedError` (CP-M6-1-3-local class) · `ChannelConditionInvalidError` (§4.3 vocabulary discipline — orchestrator refuses backend returns outside CHANNEL_CONDITION_VALUES).
