# Permitted choices and reserved decisions
**Permitted within the adopted direction:** block order within the policy's lead/then sequence; wording under the role cap; which deltas to narrate and which to leave in the receipts; which receipt to list first; title wording.
**Escalate to the commissioning operator:** overriding the page's lead move; a page state with silence but no way to say what closes it; a pending item with a missing required field (seat, due, mechanism, brief_ref, watch_ref).
**Reserved, never a skill output:** any directive phrasing; any cause without a causal receipt; any trigger not backed by a pending decision, an advisory brief or a Watch record; any individual-level field on an aggregate-only page; serving a stub page state; changing a role's voice or cap (policy, by correction).
Outputs are content records, previews and review questions. There is no decision output type.
