THE ANSWER IN BRIEF · R03/R04
The estate has valuable language assets, but its eligible corpus is not yet certified
SITUATION
The authorised staged subset contains 529.34 audio hours and 3,958 files. A separate transcript build contains 157,513 natural text units. These are candidate inventories, not an estate-wide qualified total.
COMPLICATION
Language-folder labels do not prove linguistic purity. Music exclusion, independent quality distributions, audio–text alignment and onward rights remain unresolved.
QUESTION
What should be protected, qualified and productised—and what can responsibly be offered to an AI lab?
ANSWER
Treat estate stewardship, corpus qualification and product development as separate workstreams. Pilot controlled-access products while building a defensible data card; do not sell a storage total as training-ready language data.
