## 5. Decision request format for later implementation

Each unresolved request will identify: exact clause/source; existing ruling checked; precise contradiction or unfilled choice; recommended interpretation and alternatives; affected package/claim; behaviour while held; and the authority required to settle it. Bundle related questions at their dependency gate, rather than repeatedly asking for global approval or silently deciding governance in code.
