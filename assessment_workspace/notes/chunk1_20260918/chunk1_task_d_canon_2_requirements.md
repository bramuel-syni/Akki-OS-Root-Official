## Part I · The Promise

- **C2-R-001 · CIF is the sixth engine, peer to five.** Peer to Northena/SyniSense/Solva/Mtafiti/Targeta, subordinate to none; broadest reach: build loop, training path, planning, proposal generation, wherever system or people render a judgement. *Rationale:* peer-status is structural. *Canon 2 §1.*
- **C2-R-002 · CIF is in force from adoption · no phasing.** Every operation in every engine, from its first occurrence, executes under CIF spec. *Rationale:* no ramp-up or activation sequence. *Canon 2 §1.*
- **C2-R-003 · Corrections must compound, not evaporate.** Correction made once retires the class of error, not the instance. *Rationale:* removes re-litigation-forever failure class. *Canon 2 §2.*
- **C2-R-004 · Deferred ideas must remain retrievable when conditions arrive.** Rejected/deferred/half-explored ideas wait with what they need named, and return when their conditions arrive. *Rationale:* prevents good work dying of timing. *Canon 2 §2.*
- **C2-R-005 · Four-way stated promise.** Stop repeating yourself · shown confidence can be trusted · ideas stop dying with the session · insights spread everywhere they apply. *Rationale:* the promise register for CIF. *Canon 2 §3.*
- **C2-R-006 · Adoption-time measure: human corrects less each cycle for same/better output.** Any stronger claim is made only on cycles it has actually been measured. *Rationale:* one-metric commitment at adoption. *Canon 2 §3.*

## Part II · The Governing Principle

- **C2-R-007 · Architecture is invention; components are known.** Components deliberately not novel — known gates, engines, code structures, each with defined tested conditions of success. *Rationale:* build-in-known-mechanics discipline. *Canon 2 §4.*
- **C2-R-008 · Single-experiment principle applied to CIF.** Only the assembled system is under experiment; nothing beneath is experimental — CIF enters in force without qualification. *Rationale:* CIF is not a pilot. *Canon 2 §5.*
- **C2-R-009 · Gates never bind existence or force.** Gates bind spend, output quality, or a claim — never existence or force. *Rationale:* mirrors D1 §4.12 doctrine. *Canon 2 §5.*
- **C2-R-010 · Construction order carries no epistemic weight.** Nothing about when a piece of CIF was built bears on whether it is true. *Rationale:* code-history-free truth. *Canon 2 §5.*
- **C2-R-011 · Coach seat is constitutionally load-bearing.** The coach — teacher, auditor, informer — whose corrections strike the assumptions a verdict rests on, and who alone may adopt a new frame; no configuration operates without this seat filled. *Rationale:* human role is permanent, not transitional. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-012 · Solva-pattern validation applied to CIF's own claims.** Claims stated only at their evidence class; conclusion's support meets its bar; contradictions surfaced not averaged; refusal names reason + what would satisfy it. CIF's own claims evaluated by this discipline exactly as any answer. *Rationale:* no leniency for the faculty. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-013 · Critic Seam extension for CIF pre-ship.** Critic Seam is the standing quality layer for worker output (Doc 7). CIF's pre-ship checklist extends that rubric to idea selection — amendment, not parallel. *Rationale:* extension, not proliferation. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-014 · Independence rule.** A reviewing instance must differ genuinely from what it reviews — a different base model, or a materially different context shape. Same-base/same-context review shares blind spots. *Rationale:* independence must be structural, not nominal. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-015 · Run telemetry is the binding surface for CIF parameters.** CIF monitoring parameters bind to named fields in per-run measurement stream. *Rationale:* CIF has no private telemetry. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-016 · Watch findings metabolised like any ground reality.** Watch detection findings are a source CIF may metabolise; Watch never certifies its own framing; fitness counted in demonstrated firings, never self-graded value. *Rationale:* mutual peerage + self-certification bound. *Canon 2 §6.*
- **C2-R-017 · Engine walls hold under CIF.** Planner's learning never widens what an objective may reach; critic detects and never decides; CIF operates inside these walls everywhere it acts. *Rationale:* the walls are absolute for the faculty. *Canon 2 §6.*

## Part III · Vocabulary

- **C2-R-018 · Idea = any candidate the faculty evaluates or picks.** Proposal, technique, product move, interpretation, training choice, ordering decision, selection among alternatives. *Rationale:* fixes scope of Idea. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-019 · Verdict = evaluated position on an idea.** Adopt · reject · rank · defer — rendered against a stated goal. *Rationale:* one-of-four verdict types. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-020 · Manifest = load-bearing assumptions a verdict rests on.** Each entry classed fact/recalled/inferred, elicited by "what, if false, flips this?". *Rationale:* fixes manifest shape and elicitation question. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-021 · Flip = verdict reversing because information struck a manifest entry.** Whether newly arrived or already held and only newly attended to. *Rationale:* fixes flip semantics. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-022 · Frame = thinking structure shared across many verdicts.** The shape of the calculation, not a variable inside it. *Rationale:* differentiates frame from verdict. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-023 · Three frame signatures.** Cascade (one strike flips many verdicts) · absorption (same correction class patched repeatedly while underlying pattern persists) · misfit (info reduces to no existing structure, contradicts several standing positions, or resists manifest probe). *Rationale:* names the three detection patterns. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-024 · Metabolisation = complete digestion of an adopted frame.** Across everything the overturned structure touched. *Rationale:* definition of frame-adoption completeness. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-025 · Four archive states.** Adopted · not-yet-conditioned (missing condition named) · unverified-provisional (no verdict returned) · superseded. Failure is a state an idea can hold, never an identity it acquires. *Rationale:* fixes the four-state archive vocabulary. *Canon 2 §7.*
- **C2-R-026 · Cycle = working period between owner touchpoints.** The measurement unit for the faculty's own parameters. *Rationale:* defines the CIF clock. *Canon 2 §7.*

## Part IV · Core Practice

- **C2-R-027 · Every shipped verdict + every idea pick carries its manifest.** An unmanifested verdict is a defect, not an oversight to note and move past. *Rationale:* zero-tolerance rule. *Canon 2 §8.*
- **C2-R-028 · Entries are evidence-classed.** A verdict resting on inferred entries states its provisionality where it ships. *Rationale:* provisionality visible at point-of-ship. *Canon 2 §8.*
- **C2-R-029 · Selections record why the winner ranked first.** The pick carries its own manifest, so selection bias is examinable. *Rationale:* selection = a verdict with its own manifest. *Canon 2 §8.*
- **C2-R-030 · Silence is a data state, never confirmation.** A response receiving no verdict from a human is archived as unverified-provisional, never counted as agreed. *Rationale:* prevents silent-implied-approval. *Canon 2 §8.*
- **C2-R-031 · Every evaluated idea lands in an archive state the same cycle.** Nothing exits silently. *Rationale:* enforces §9 living-archive completeness. *Canon 2 §9.*
- **C2-R-032 · Circumstantial rejection names missing condition specifically.** Not "not ready" — what, precisely, would make it ready. *Rationale:* rejections must be re-openable by structural signal. *Canon 2 §9.*
- **C2-R-033 · Every entry carries rent (review-by + re-qualification budget).** An expired entry is re-qualified or superseded, never left to sit. *Rationale:* archive doesn't rot. *Canon 2 §9.*
- **C2-R-034 · Resurfaced idea enters with zero inherited credibility.** New candidate, fully re-qualified against current goal, not restored to prior standing. *Rationale:* forbids implicit-credit-carry-over. *Canon 2 §9.*
- **C2-R-035 · Re-qualification is paper-first.** Re-scoring under changed assumptions precedes compute; simulation runs only where paper verdict warrants spend. *Rationale:* cost-discipline on re-qualification. *Canon 2 §9.*
- **C2-R-036 · Naivety pass.** On demand or on stagnation, candidates re-qualified with inherited constraints struck ("known not to work", "nobody does it this way", "too simple to matter") while ground-truth constraints stay (law, physics, custody, budget); every struck constraint gets a short evidence brief; pass never classifies autonomously; survivor never ships without autopsy attached. *Rationale:* structured way to surface high-bias rejections. *Canon 2 §10.1.*
- **C2-R-037 · Pre-ship checklist compiled from coach's correction history.** Replay-only, never claims to cover novel classes, enters Critic Seam standing rubric as amendment not parallel mechanism. *Rationale:* pre-ship is derivative + additive. *Canon 2 §10.2.*

## Part V · The Frame Lifecycle

- **C2-R-038 · Three frame signatures watched continuously from manifest corpus.** Standing practice, not periodic review; observed signature escalates as frame-candidate in same cycle. *Rationale:* signature detection is continuous. *Canon 2 §11.*
- **C2-R-039 · Faculty detects, never adopts a frame on its own signature.** *Rationale:* detection ≠ decision, restated for CIF. *Canon 2 §11.*
- **C2-R-040 · Origination port is coach / ground reality / genuinely outside reviewer only.** Constitutionally load-bearing, permanent in every configuration. *Rationale:* frame origination is human/reality-only. *Canon 2 §12.*
- **C2-R-041 · Metabolisation follows four ordered mandatory steps.** (1) Adoption record names the overturned structure (not just the new statement) · (2) Every archived verdict whose manifest touches overturned structure re-qualifies on paper; sweep completes before any new verdict on affected class · (3) Adopted frame enters manifest vocabulary so subsequent verdicts declare position relative to it · (4) Closure holds when absorption signature quiet for three cycles; noisy = incomplete, sweep re-runs. *Rationale:* fixes the sweep discipline. *Canon 2 §13.*
- **C2-R-042 · Frame-adoption commit without sweep record fails build check.** Same discipline applied to a function landing with no registry row. *Rationale:* metabolisation enforcement structural. *Canon 2 §13.*

## Part VI · Monitoring

- **C2-R-043 · Three monitoring capabilities operate under defined parameters.** None is an entry condition for the faculty; all three measured like every quality parameter. *Rationale:* capability-level parity. *Canon 2 §14.*
- **C2-R-044 · Condition watchers bind to named live telemetry fields.** Unbindable condition falls to rent-date human review instead; fired watcher re-queues its idea as candidate, never adopts on its own. *Rationale:* automation is scoped + non-adoption. *Canon 2 §14.*
- **C2-R-045 · Cascade detection = multi-flip identification across manifest corpus.** Escalates as frame-candidate finding under same rule as any detected signature. *Rationale:* uses the standard escalation path. *Canon 2 §14.*
- **C2-R-046 · Adversarial manifest elicitation via second-instance interrogation.** Satisfies independence rule — genuinely different reviewer, never same-base echo. *Rationale:* second-instance = adversarial by design. *Canon 2 §14.*

## Part VII · The Permanent Bound

- **C2-R-047 · Self-certification closed by construction.** No parameter set exists whose truth would license the faculty to validate its own framing; any such evidence would be evaluated inside the frame it was meant to certify. *Rationale:* structural prohibition on self-certification. *Canon 2 §15.*
- **C2-R-048 · Self-certification proposals are rejected on sight.** Not a feature request; an exhibit of the exact failure this bound exists to prevent. *Rationale:* names the proposal class. *Canon 2 §15.*
- **C2-R-049 · Function self-certification would fill is held by a composite.** Faculty detects and metabolises · coach originates and ratifies · Solva-pattern validation keeps evidence honest throughout. Loop closes through the coach seat, never around it. *Rationale:* the three-way composite is the substitute. *Canon 2 §15.*

## Part VIII · Operative and Quality Parameters

- **C2-R-050 · Operative parameter · Manifest coverage.** 100% of shipped verdicts and picks carry a manifest. FACT. *Rationale:* fixed operating constant. *Canon 2 §16.*
- **C2-R-051 · Operative parameter · Archive completeness.** Every evaluated idea lands in a state the same cycle it was evaluated. FACT. *Canon 2 §16.*
- **C2-R-052 · Operative parameter · Escalation latency.** Frame signature escalates in the cycle it is observed. FACT. *Canon 2 §16.*
- **C2-R-053 · Operative parameter · Metabolisation execution.** Four-step sweep triggers within one cycle of adoption; completes before new verdicts issue on affected class. FACT. *Canon 2 §16.*
- **C2-R-054 · Operative parameter · Rent collection.** Archive review-by dates honoured; expired entries re-qualified or superseded, never left standing. FACT. *Canon 2 §16.*
- **C2-R-055 · Quality parameter · Promise metric.** Owner-confirmed repeat-class corrections decline across cycles; flat/rising for two consecutive cycles triggers a CIF review. *Rationale:* the CIF outcome metric. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-056 · Quality parameter · Manifest hit-rate.** When a flip occurs, the struck assumption was already in the manifest ≥ 70% DEFAULT; below bar, elicitation protocol repaired. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-057 · Quality parameter · Provisional honesty.** Zero instances of inferred-footing content shipped into the register of fact; one instance is a finding on record. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-058 · Quality parameter · Metabolisation closure.** Absorption signature for adopted frame quiet within 3 cycles DEFAULT; noisy = incomplete, sweep re-runs. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-059 · Quality parameter · Overhead ceiling.** CIF practice consumes no more than owner-set share of cycle budget; breach shrinks CIF, never product work. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-060 · Quality parameter · False-escalation rate.** Frame candidates later ruled non-events ≤ 30% DEFAULT; above bar, detection thresholds tighten. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-061 · DEFAULT values are operating constants.** Cheap to correct, revised on evidence at cycle boundary, never mid-cycle, never require reopening this specification. *Rationale:* DEFAULT ≠ config-flag. *Canon 2 §17.*
- **C2-R-062 · Breach acts on CIF's own practice.** Never resolved by relaxing product work elsewhere. *Rationale:* breach-locus is inside CIF. *Canon 2 §17.*

## Part IX · Enforcement

- **C2-R-063 · CIF uses only standing enforcement vehicles — no new mechanisms.** A faculty needing its own enforcement machinery would itself be structural proliferation. *Rationale:* mirrors D1 §4.6. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-064 · Manifests enforced by schema-required fields.** Unmanifested verdict rejected at submission; standing format-gate pattern; form-only check. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-065 · Archive entries are append-only ledger rows.** Standing query surfaces any evaluated-but-unarchived idea as a finding. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-066 · CIF claims evaluated by the assertion boundary.** No separate leniency for the faculty that watches everyone else. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-067 · Metabolisation enforced by build-check.** Frame-adoption commit lacking sweep record fails build check; same pattern as function-with-no-registry-row. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-068 · Rent + monitoring: queryable fields + standing-query surfacing.** Same machinery as everything else. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-069 · Selection defects enter Critic Seam's rubric as a named amendment class.** Not a parallel checklist. *Canon 2 §18.*
- **C2-R-070 · Ephemeral layer audited, not policed in flight.** Live conversational reasoning (judgement spoken before it becomes an artifact) is governed as behavioural discipline with mechanical audit backstop, not intercepted mid-thought; everything that lands is gated. *Rationale:* draws the audit/enforcement line at artifact-boundary. *Canon 2 §18.*

## Part X · Governance

- **C2-R-071 · CIF yield counted in owner-confirmed catches only.** Self-graded value is inadmissible. *Rationale:* structural bar on self-scored value. *Canon 2 §19.*
- **C2-R-072 · No CIF output claims coach-independence, in any configuration, permanently.** Origination port is load-bearing every time. *Canon 2 §19.*
- **C2-R-073 · CIF is entry #1 in its own archive.** Manifested, rented, revisable under exactly the rules it applies to everything else. *Rationale:* self-application. *Canon 2 §19.*
- **C2-R-074 · Paper precedes compute; detection never decides; engine walls hold everywhere CIF operates — with no CIF exception.** *Canon 2 §19.*
- **C2-R-075 · What CIF does NOT do (§20).** Does not originate frames, adjudicate its own framing, or replace the coach seat; does not block work, hold veto authority, or acquire a review layer above itself; there is no meta-CIF, and proposing one is itself a frame-absorption finding; does not promise a zero residual — value is stated as halving the residual, never eliminating it. *Rationale:* explicit scope-creep guard. *Canon 2 §20.*
