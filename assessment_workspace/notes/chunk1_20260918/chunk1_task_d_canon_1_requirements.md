## Part I · What Akki Is

- **C1-R-001 · Custody-platform installation shape.** Akki installs inside an organisation's own perimeter and is the layer through which every AI operation touching that organisation's data must pass. *Rationale:* frames the platform as an in-perimeter chokepoint, not a SaaS product. *Canon 1 §1.*
- **C1-R-002 · Four custody duties.** The platform holds the estate under declared controls, maintains the record of what the estate contains, settles commissioned work against that record, and produces evidence on demand to the organisation and to anyone the organisation must answer to. *Rationale:* fixes the four-verb duty of a custodian applied to data. *Canon 1 §1.*
- **C1-R-003 · Negative identity.** Akki is not a model, not a wrapper around a model, and not an assistant; it is the layer beneath every model and every application, and it is the only path between them and the estate. *Rationale:* forbids product-repositioning that would blur custody duties. *Canon 1 §1.*
- **C1-R-004 · Single-tenant by construction.** One organisation, one installation, one record; no multi-tenant deployment mode and no shared control plane. *Rationale:* structurally prevents cross-tenant leakage. *Canon 1 §1.*
- **C1-R-005 · Estate content determines capability activation.** The estate's contents — never the customer's identity — determine which capabilities activate. *Rationale:* prevents customer-specific specialisation entering platform spec. *Canon 1 §1.*
- **C1-R-006 · Estate scale invariance.** An estate is an estate at every scale (organisation, department, archive, single file); the same operations apply at every scale. *Rationale:* rules out scale-specific mechanisms that don't hold at every zoom level. *Canon 1 §1.1.*
- **C1-R-007 · Four verbs cover the whole platform.** Every capability is one of Measure / Govern / Extract / Prove or a composition of them. *Rationale:* rejects capabilities that don't compose from these four. *Canon 1 §1.2.*
- **C1-R-008 · Measure: unmeasured is unmeasured.** Establish what the estate contains — composition, condition, language, sensitivity, rights — and state plainly which regions have not been measured; an unmeasured region is reported as unmeasured, never as empty and never as clean. *Rationale:* absence of measurement is a fact, not a null. *Canon 1 §1.2 · verb "Measure".*
- **C1-R-009 · Govern: rules apply on every operation.** Enforce, on every operation, the rules the organisation has declared: what may be read, by whom, for what purpose, under what masking, with what evidence required before an answer may be asserted. *Rationale:* rules are executable on every call, not documented statements. *Canon 1 §1.2 · verb "Govern".*
- **C1-R-010 · Extract: commissioned + priced before running.** Convert estate material into qualified facts, trained models, licensed datasets, memory available to applications — as commissioned work priced before it runs. *Rationale:* enforces commercial ceremony precedes execution. *Canon 1 §1.2 · verb "Extract".*
- **C1-R-011 · Prove: every operation gets an externally-verifiable receipt.** Emit, for every operation, a receipt that an outside party can verify without an account on the system and without the operator's cooperation. *Rationale:* audit-independence is structural. *Canon 1 §1.2 · verb "Prove".*
- **C1-R-012 · Four obstacles Akki removes at the layer beneath applications.** Data cannot leave · output cannot be trusted on its face · nothing is provable afterwards · rules exist on paper and nowhere else — Akki removes all four beneath the applications, above the data, so the risk function approves once. *Rationale:* fixes the problem statement Akki must satisfy. *Canon 1 §2.1–2.5.*
- **C1-R-013 · Not a model provider.** Akki does not train the frontier models it routes to and makes no claim about their internal behaviour. *Rationale:* keeps model-vendor risk external. *Canon 1 §3.*
- **C1-R-014 · Not a data warehouse.** Akki does not become the storage of record for the estate; it measures and governs access to material that remains where the organisation keeps it, except for material it is explicitly commissioned to extract and hold. *Rationale:* estate ownership stays with the organisation. *Canon 1 §3.*
- **C1-R-015 · Not a policy generator.** Rules declared in Akki are executable; a rule that cannot be enforced by the system cannot be declared in the system. *Rationale:* eliminates paper-only controls. *Canon 1 §3.*
- **C1-R-016 · Not a workflow queue.** Work is not distributed to people to perform; people hold authority, the platform performs the work. *Rationale:* prevents human-in-the-loop bottlenecks. *Canon 1 §3.*
- **C1-R-017 · Not multi-tenant (restated).** There is no mode in which two organisations' material occupies one installation. *Rationale:* reinforces §1's single-tenant guarantee. *Canon 1 §3.*
- **C1-R-018 · Not the end-user's product.** No platform decision optimises an end-user's experience directly; end-user experience belongs to the applications built on the platform. *Rationale:* preserves single-durable-approval by keeping UX-specific compromises out of platform spec. *Canon 1 §3.*

## Part II · Binding Doctrine (the 12 doctrines)

- **C1-R-019 · Doctrine 4.1 — Fail-closed custody.** If a custody control cannot execute, the operation does not happen; enforced structurally with no configuration flag, no code-path bypass, and an automated build gate that fails the build if a path around the control is introduced. *Rationale:* custody failure = refuse; not degrade. *Canon 1 §4.1.*
- **C1-R-020 · Doctrine 4.1 — Fail-open cost, fail-closed custody.** Custody controls fail closed; cost optimisations fail transparent; a component existing to reduce cost must never halt a governed operation by failing. *Rationale:* separates safety-class failures from performance-class failures. *Canon 1 §4.1.*
- **C1-R-021 · Doctrine 4.2 — Unrepresentable, not policed.** Where a state must never occur, the system is built so the state cannot be expressed (function signature, type, schema); reviewed prohibitions are eventually violated. *Rationale:* structural impossibility beats vigilance. *Canon 1 §4.2.*
- **C1-R-022 · Doctrine 4.2 · Biometric-template register has no field for such a value.** The register that could hold individual-level identification biometrics — voiceprint, faceprint, any body-identifying template — has no field in which such a value could be placed. *Rationale:* biometric material is unrepresentable at the register level. *Canon 1 §4.2.*
- **C1-R-023 · Doctrine 4.2 · No confidence parameter on the claim function.** The function computing what an answer may claim has no parameter through which a model's confidence in itself can enter. *Rationale:* prevents self-reported confidence from bounding claims. *Canon 1 §4.2.*
- **C1-R-024 · Doctrine 4.2 · Watch behavioural-conformance parameters live under a different owner.** Behavioural-conformance parameters held by the Watch under its own custody doctrine are a different object with a different owner (Document 8); neither is ever a field in the other's register. *Rationale:* separates governance-of-claim from security-of-behaviour. *Canon 1 §4.2.*
- **C1-R-025 · Doctrine 4.3 — One record.** There is exactly one record; nothing maintains a second; every admission, measurement, commission, operation, answer, refusal, rule change, and access grant writes to a single append-only ledger. *Rationale:* single-source-of-truth for audit. *Canon 1 §4.3.*
- **C1-R-026 · Doctrine 4.3 · Scratch is not record.** Working state — a partial count mid-census, a draft not yet committed — is scratch, not record; it becomes record only when finalised and stamped, and the moment of stamping is itself recorded. *Rationale:* protects the ledger from in-flight-noise pollution. *Canon 1 §4.3.*
- **C1-R-027 · Doctrine 4.4 — Measure, do not assert.** No figure enters the system as a claim; every figure carries how it was obtained; vendor benchmarks and published literature never substitute for measurement. *Rationale:* platform figures must be organisation-owned. *Canon 1 §4.4.*
- **C1-R-028 · Doctrine 4.4 · Estimates render as estimates on the number wherever shown.** An estimate renders as an estimate, on the number, wherever it appears. *Rationale:* forbids stripping provenance in UI/reports. *Canon 1 §4.4.*
- **C1-R-029 · Doctrine 4.5 — Single owner per value.** Every value is computed once, by one component, and carried forward as fact; a downstream component does not recompute, re-validate, or second-guess. *Rationale:* redoing upstream work creates two sources for one value = defect. *Canon 1 §4.5.*
- **C1-R-030 · Doctrine 4.6 — Extension, not proliferation.** New capability extends an existing component; it does not create a sibling. *Rationale:* prevents parallel registries / parallel ledgers with parallel approval paths. *Canon 1 §4.6.*
- **C1-R-031 · Doctrine 4.6 · Test for sibling defect.** A proposed component needing its own record, its own rules, and its own approval path is a sibling and is wrong. *Rationale:* structural test for proliferation. *Canon 1 §4.6.*
- **C1-R-032 · Doctrine 4.7 — Authority is held by people; work is done by the platform.** No person is placed in the runtime path of an operation; people declare rules, approve rule sets, set escalation criteria, and disposition exceptions. *Rationale:* removes humans from per-operation critical path. *Canon 1 §4.7.*
- **C1-R-033 · Doctrine 4.7 · Repeated-approver signal.** If the same class of item repeatedly reaches an approver, the criteria are wrong — the response is to redefine them, never to process the queue faster. *Rationale:* structural feedback rule against approver saturation. *Canon 1 §4.7.*
- **C1-R-034 · Doctrine 4.8 — Inheritance.** Everything built inside Akki, or integrated into it, inherits its custody; there is no other way to build. *Rationale:* single-approval covers all applications above. *Canon 1 §4.8.*
- **C1-R-035 · Doctrine 4.8 · Central-claim break test.** If any application can reach the estate by a route that bypasses the platform, this doctrine is broken and the platform's central claim is false. *Rationale:* structural liveness check on inheritance. *Canon 1 §4.8.*
- **C1-R-036 · Doctrine 4.9 — Provenance is not optional.** A fact that cannot name its source cannot be stored; provenance is paired with a fact at the moment of writing, never attached afterwards; a fact arriving without a resolvable source is rejected at the boundary. *Rationale:* keeps the record self-verifying. *Canon 1 §4.9.*
- **C1-R-037 · Doctrine 4.9 · Capability records carry establishing work.** A record that a model can perform some task carries which work established that, on what material, when. *Rationale:* capability = a claim that must cite the work behind it. *Canon 1 §4.9.*
- **C1-R-038 · Doctrine 4.10 — Verdicts are never curated.** Inputs, models, and corpora may be engineered without limit; the evidence a verdict is measured against may not be. *Rationale:* prevents rehearsed-checkpoint hazard. *Canon 1 §4.10.*
- **C1-R-039 · Doctrine 4.10 · Uncurated-composition sampling.** Every validating sample is drawn from measured, uncurated composition and published internally whatever it says. *Rationale:* prevents selection bias on the eval axis. *Canon 1 §4.10.*
- **C1-R-040 · Doctrine 4.11 — Canon before ruling.** No decision proceeds from memory or summary where the governing document exists; unread canon is read before ruling; a rule fires against the rule set on disk, not against recollection. *Rationale:* binds both platform and its human governors. *Canon 1 §4.11.*
- **C1-R-041 · Doctrine 4.12 — Experimentation exists at one level only.** The assembled system is the sole object under test; no component beneath it is experimental; every mechanic deploys in force with conditions of success implemented and its quality measured, or its parameters are undefined — never runs tentatively. *Rationale:* forbids pilot/shadow/observe-first modes for fully-specified mechanisms. *Canon 1 §4.12.*
- **C1-R-042 · Doctrine 4.12 · Gates never bind whether a specified mechanism runs at all.** Gates bind spend, output quality, or a claim about the platform; they never bind whether a fully specified mechanism runs at all. *Rationale:* clarifies gate function is bounding, not conditional execution. *Canon 1 §4.12.*
- **C1-R-043 · §5 Precedence — hierarchy of authority.** Precedence order top-to-bottom: (1) Doctrine, (2) The Promise Register (Part VI), (3) This canon's specifications, (4) Implementation convenience (never decisive). *Rationale:* precedence rule for conflicts. *Canon 1 §5.*
- **C1-R-044 · §5 · Shipped divergence is not evidence.** Where implementation cost is genuinely prohibitive, specification is amended through ceremony before anything ships — a shipped divergence is never retrospective evidence the specification was wrong. *Rationale:* prevents shipped-code overriding spec. *Canon 1 §5.*
- **C1-R-045 · §5 · Two-authorities conflict resolution.** Within the security domain the Watch canon governs; within the governance domain this canon and its companions govern; at every seam, the peerage clauses of Document 8 and the matrix of Document 9 decide. *Rationale:* fixes Watch/governance authority boundary. *Canon 1 §5.*
- **C1-R-046 · §5 · Unresolved-conflict escalation.** A conflict surviving Doctrine + Promises + Canon specifications is a specification defect and is escalated — never resolved by whichever document was read most recently. *Rationale:* forbids most-recent-document-wins resolution. *Canon 1 §5.*

## Part III · Architecture

- **C1-R-047 · Deployment topology.** Akki installs inside the organisation's perimeter (data centre, private cloud, own cloud tenancy); organisation controls infrastructure, network boundary, and identity system; Syni operates the software. *Rationale:* clear operator/customer split. *Canon 1 §6.*
- **C1-R-048 · §6 · Exactly one outbound path.** Exactly one path leaves the perimeter (the custody boundary); nothing else in the platform holds outbound network capability, enforced by network-layer allowlist + static-analysis build gate. *Rationale:* single-egress guarantee is enforceable at build time. *Canon 1 §6.*
- **C1-R-049 · §6 · Compute stays in-perimeter.** Models hosted by the organisation and GPU capacity the platform allocates run inside the perimeter; where an external frontier model is genuinely required, the custody boundary governs the single crossing. *Rationale:* compute-in-perimeter default, frontier-crossing exception. *Canon 1 §6.*
- **C1-R-050 · §7 · Six engines on three orthogonal axes.** The platform is six engines (Northena · SyniSense · Solva · Mtafiti · Targeta · CIF) governing exactly one thing on three orthogonal axes (Direction · Boundary · Depth); a governor performing another's function creates a single point at which run can be admitted, bounded, and asserted by the same logic. *Rationale:* separation-of-governance-concerns is structural. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-051 · §7 · Northena is deterministic by construction.** Whether a run is still in scope, and whether it is done — deterministic by construction, no model, no learned weights, no adaptive behaviour. *Rationale:* direction engine cannot drift. *Canon 1 §7 · engine table.*
- **C1-R-052 · §7 · SyniSense = single chokepoint through which every outbound model call transits.** *Rationale:* boundary is one point, not a policy. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-053 · §7 · Solva is the one reasoning engine — with tightest structural leash.** *Rationale:* reasoning carries the highest defensibility risk. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-054 · §7 · Mtafiti governs measurement.** What the estate contains, and how defensibly each part can be relied upon. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-055 · §7 · Targeta governs where to mine and in what order.** Within a budget, without ever narrowing what an objective may reach. *Rationale:* budget-bounded but not scope-narrowing. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-056 · §7 · CIF governs judgement.** How the system's own judgements are made, retained, and improved — peer to the five, subordinate to none, specified in Document 2. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-057 · §7 · Six-into-two-services composition.** The six engines compose into two services connected by two named seams (specified in Document 4): a build-the-asset service (census + extraction ending in a single frozen unit contract) and a serve-an-objective service (calls back only for what has not yet converged). *Rationale:* names the system-level composition. *Canon 1 §7.*
- **C1-R-058 · §7.1 · Two authorities — governance + Watch — are peer.** The six engines constitute the governing authority ("was this permitted, and is it right?"); the Watch is the security authority ("is this intended, and authentic?") peer and not subordinate; Watch is not a seventh engine and not a layer above the six. *Rationale:* structural peerage. *Canon 1 §7.1.*
- **C1-R-059 · §7.1 · Cross-authority mutual consumption + no command.** Neither authority commands the other; each consumes what the other produces; every task executes under exactly one authority's identity. *Rationale:* separation-of-command, mutual-evidence. *Canon 1 §7.1.*
- **C1-R-060 · §7.1 · Watch containment acts first on cross-authority disagreement.** Where an operation is permitted but not intended, the Watch's containment acts first (bounded and reversible) and a person resolves the conflict; the disagreement itself is recorded. *Rationale:* Watch has structural first-move on ambiguity. *Canon 1 §7.1.*
- **C1-R-061 · §7.1 · Mkondo Charter (Document 9) is where identity/record/handoff live.** The record classes each authority writes, the identity every task class executes under, and the contracts by which work crosses between them are held in the orchestration charter (Document 9). *Rationale:* fixes single-place-answers for who-writes/reads/acts questions. *Canon 1 §7.1.*
- **C1-R-062 · §8 · Function & Promise Registry is one canonical versioned artifact.** Registers every named function in the platform — gate, engine behaviour, worker obligation, console guarantee — with the promise it protects and its full accounting. Every function belongs to one of the six engines or to a named surface for interface-level guarantees. Specified in full in Document 7. *Rationale:* one registry, versioned, one place. *Canon 1 §8.*
- **C1-R-063 · §8 · Both-way registry test.** No gate exists without an honestly statable promise; no promise exists that traces to no function protecting it. *Rationale:* bidirectional consistency check. *Canon 1 §8.*
- **C1-R-064 · §8 · Function that fails both tests is retired.** A function that cannot answer both questions is retired, not preserved out of caution. *Rationale:* explicitly kills orphan functions. *Canon 1 §8.*

## Part IV · The Estate

- **C1-R-065 · §9 · Estate is a single measurable object at every scale.** *Rationale:* scale-invariance restated for measurement. *Canon 1 §9.*
- **C1-R-066 · §9.1 · Three states of every estate region.** Every region is in exactly one of: `Measured — sound` / `Measured — degraded (limitation named)` / `Not yet measured` — rendered with equal prominence wherever the estate is described. *Rationale:* fixes the three-state vocabulary and forbids hidden-null equivalents. *Canon 1 §9.1.*
- **C1-R-067 · §9.1 · Unmeasured never renders as empty/clean/zero.** The absence of a measurement is a fact about the platform's knowledge, not a fact about the estate. *Rationale:* forbids the "0 = safe" UI class-error. *Canon 1 §9.1.*
- **C1-R-068 · §9.2 · Two-layer defensibility measurement.** Deterministic declaration applied per source (always available · the floor) + inference layer detecting content signals (contributes only after its own accuracy has been proven; until then, declaration stands alone). *Rationale:* structural separation of "learning may detect / never decide". *Canon 1 §9.2.*
- **C1-R-069 · §9.2 · Neither layer decides what a measurement means.** That verdict is always a governed, versioned lookup, never a model's output. *Rationale:* verdict is lookup, not model. *Canon 1 §9.2.*
- **C1-R-070 · §9.2 · General rule — learning may detect; it may never decide.** *Rationale:* general-purpose separation of detection from decision. *Canon 1 §9.2.*

## Part V · The Operating Loop

- **C1-R-071 · §10 · Loop shape.** Questions arrive → most answered → unanswered become ranked gaps → gaps become commissioned objectives → extraction changes what next round can answer. *Rationale:* names the platform's operating objective. *Canon 1 §10.*
- **C1-R-072 · §10 · Loop continues without any role present.** A question can arrive, fail to be answered, become a gap, and be ranked without anyone deciding it should. *Rationale:* structural liveness without human trigger. *Canon 1 §10.*
- **C1-R-073 · §10 · Record carries context that would otherwise live in someone's head.** Why a gap was filed, what was tried, what the material could not support. *Rationale:* eliminates tribal-knowledge failure class. *Canon 1 §10.*
- **C1-R-074 · §10 · Demand is discovered, not forecast.** *Rationale:* forbids advance-forecasting substituting for observation. *Canon 1 §10.*
- **C1-R-075 · §10 · Loop is self-limiting.** When the gap list stops producing high-demand candidates, the estate has been mined to the depth its current questions require. *Rationale:* mining terminates by demand, not by schedule. *Canon 1 §10.*
- **C1-R-076 · §10 · Compounding cost promise.** The second commission costs less than the first for the same value, because capability accumulates. *Rationale:* platform's economic promise on time. *Canon 1 §10.*

## Part VI · Who Akki Serves

- **C1-R-077 · §11 · Platform optimises guarantees, not end-user experience.** No platform decision optimises an end-user's experience directly; platform optimises the guarantees it issues to the applications built on it. *Rationale:* durable-approval axis. *Canon 1 §11.*
- **C1-R-078 · §12 · Six services promise register (S1–S6).** S1 (integrating application · governed intelligence surface with class/receipt/refusal semantics) · S2 (operating organisation · estate onboarded/mapped/qualified) · S3 (compliance officer · proof on demand + governed control of rules) · S4 (data buyer · verifiable intelligence products, never raw data) · S5 (infrastructure consumer · reserved · not built) · S6 (operating org + compliance officer under threat · continuous machine-speed detection + response · specified in Document 8). *Rationale:* the six-sentence promise register that constrains every function. *Canon 1 §12.*
- **C1-R-079 · §12 · S5 is reserved and unbuilt.** Registered so nothing optimises against it prematurely; not built. *Rationale:* forbids premature optimisation. *Canon 1 §12.*
- **C1-R-080 · §12 · Trace-upward requirement.** No platform function is proposed, evaluated, or justified except by tracing to one of the six sentences; a function that cannot trace upward is doing the wrong job well. *Rationale:* function-to-promise traceability is a first-order rule. *Canon 1 §12.*
- **C1-R-081 · §13 · Application definition is mechanical.** If a person who is not running the platform touches it, it is an application. *Rationale:* boundary is mechanical, not organisational. *Canon 1 §13.*
- **C1-R-082 · §13 · No privileged path for first-party surfaces.** Applications enter through the same governed boundary as any third party, hold a scoped key issued the same way, and are subject to the same custody on every call — not even for surfaces built by Syni itself. *Rationale:* forbids trusted-internal-path exception. *Canon 1 §13.*
- **C1-R-083 · §13 · Application spec lives in Document 5.** The applications an organisation actually uses (conversational surface, executive workspace, document reviewer, monitoring feed) are specified fully in Document 5. *Rationale:* Canon 5 = application spec. *Canon 1 §13.*

## Part VII · Vocabulary and Boundaries

- **C1-R-084 · §14 · Canonical terms are fixed.** The custody boundary · Estate · Commission · Objective · The record · Receipt · Console · Application — each carries the meaning stated in §14. *Rationale:* one-term-one-definition. *Canon 1 §14.*
- **C1-R-085 · §14 · Objective is frozen at admission, immutable for the run.** *Rationale:* rules out scope-drift mid-run. *Canon 1 §14.*
- **C1-R-086 · §14 · Console defined by what can be done there, never by a named person.** *Rationale:* role/console separation. *Canon 1 §14.*
- **C1-R-087 · §15 · Platform will not answer beyond its evidence.** Where material is insufficient, the platform bounds the claim or declines; it does not produce a confident answer with a caveat attached. *Rationale:* forbids caveat-hedged confident output. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-088 · §15 · No override mode.** No emergency mode, no administrator bypass, no support-access path that skips custody; a control with an exception is not a control. *Rationale:* explicit no-escape-hatch rule. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-089 · §15 · No unmeasured figures anywhere.** Not in an interface, not in a report, not in a proposal generated from it. *Rationale:* unmeasured figures cannot escape UI or report path. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-090 · §15 · No second record anywhere.** No component keeps its own account of what happened, however convenient. *Rationale:* single-ledger restated at Part VII. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-091 · §15 · No person in the runtime path.** Authority is exercised over rules and exceptions, never over individual operations as a matter of course. *Rationale:* runtime-path exclusion of humans. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-092 · §15 · Platform will not know buyer, price, or order.** Commerce is application territory; the platform governs access to output and records every egress; it charges nothing and transacts with no one. *Rationale:* commerce is out of platform scope. *Canon 1 §15.*
- **C1-R-093 · §16 · Nine-document canon shape.** This canon (D1) is the foundation; eight further documents complete the specification, and each assumes D1. *Rationale:* fixes the canon set as nine docs. *Canon 1 §16.*
- **C1-R-094 · §16 · D1 governs on contradiction.** Where any of the eight further documents appears to contradict Document 1, Document 1 governs. *Rationale:* D1 = precedence-top among the nine. *Canon 1 §16.*
