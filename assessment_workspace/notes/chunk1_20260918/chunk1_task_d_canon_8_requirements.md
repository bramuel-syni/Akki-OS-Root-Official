## PART I · The Promise

### §1 What the Watch Is

- **C8-R-001 · Watch is the platform's security authority.** The Watch is the platform's security authority. *Rationale:* fixes identity. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-002 · Peer to governing authority, subordinate to nothing.** The Watch stands beside the six engines as a peer, subordinate to nothing. *Rationale:* peer status is constitutional. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-003 · Answers the intent/identity/authenticity question.** The Watch answers: not "was this permitted" but "was this intended, and is this actor, input, and action what it claims to be?" *Rationale:* delimits Watch's question. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-004 · Watch covers the gap gates cannot see.** Every gate can pass, every receipt can be clean, and damage can still be happening — a valid credential misused, a poisoned context, a purpose laundered across lawful calls — and the Watch exists for exactly that gap. *Rationale:* fixes the class of attacks Watch owns. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-005 · Watch promise (four properties).** Continuous, machine-speed detection and response against misuse; invisible in legitimate use; incapable of acting unobserved; extracting intelligence from every attack it defeats; proving its own fitness on a standing cadence without human effort. *Rationale:* fixes the Watch's four promise properties. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-006 · Every Watch function traces to §1.** Every Watch function traces to the promise sentence of §1, exactly as every governance function traces to a service sentence of Canon 1 §12. *Rationale:* traceability to the promise. *Canon 8 §1 · cross-ref Canon 1 §12.*
- **C8-R-007 · Every Part IX parameter protects a §1 clause.** Every parameter in Part IX protects a clause of §1. *Rationale:* parameters have promise-anchors. *Canon 8 §1.*
- **C8-R-008 · No external justification of Watch functions.** Nothing in the Watch is proposed, evaluated, or justified by anything else. *Rationale:* self-contained justification. *Canon 8 §1.*

### §2 Why It Exists

- **C8-R-009 · The platform's promise invites the test.** A product that declares custody and provability invites the test — ethical and hostile alike. *Rationale:* provocation is structural. *Canon 8 §2.*
- **C8-R-010 · Three attack shapes drove design.** Three attack shapes drove every structural decision: machine-speed attack (over before humans wake); slow play (drifts beneath every threshold); authorised attack (never breaks a rule). *Rationale:* fixes the threat model. *Canon 8 §2.*
- **C8-R-011 · Machine-speed answer = autonomy safe by construction.** Answer to machine-speed attack: autonomy made safe by construction. *Rationale:* fixes design axis for W3. *Canon 8 §2.*
- **C8-R-012 · Slow-play answer = the strata of Part IV.** Answer to slow play: floor that cannot learn, cohort that is hard to boil, signature that cannot be stolen, accumulation view that reads patience back at the attacker. *Rationale:* fixes strata origin. *Canon 8 §2.*
- **C8-R-013 · Authorised-attack answer = declared intent reconciled against realised conduct.** Answer to authorised attack: declared intent, reconciled against realised conduct. *Rationale:* fixes reconciler origin. *Canon 8 §2.*

### §3 What the Watch Is Not (4 exclusions)

- **C8-R-014 · Not a seventh engine.** The Watch is not a seventh engine; the six engines are the governing authority, the Watch is the second authority beside it, and it performs none of their functions and none of them performs its. *Rationale:* prevents merger into engines. *Canon 8 §3.*
- **C8-R-015 · Not a layer above governance.** The Watch is not a layer above governance; a second governance layer would be D5 meta-spiral. *Rationale:* Watch escapes D5 by different question/clock. *Canon 8 §3 · cross-ref Canon 7 §47 D5.*
- **C8-R-016 · Watch never converges and never concludes.** Governance converges run by run; the Watch never converges and never concludes. *Rationale:* distinct clocks prevent D5. *Canon 8 §3.*
- **C8-R-017 · Watch holds no authority over governance verdict.** The Watch holds no authority over any governance verdict. *Rationale:* peer separation. *Canon 8 §3.*
- **C8-R-018 · Not an observer.** The Watch acts autonomously within Part VI bounds; observe-first deployment is prohibited by W4. *Rationale:* prevents watch-only mode. *Canon 8 §3 · cross-ref §4.4 W4.*
- **C8-R-019 · Not a data consumer.** The Watch reads evidence and behaviour to protect the estate; it never feeds analytics, scoring, pricing, or management visibility. *Rationale:* fixes non-analytical use. *Canon 8 §3.*
- **C8-R-020 · Watch profile store is armory only.** The Watch's one profile store is armory under W13, renderable to no surface. *Rationale:* profile isolation from surfaces. *Canon 8 §3 · cross-ref §4.13 W13.*

## PART II · The Doctrines (W1–W14)

### §4 Fourteen Doctrines

- **C8-R-021 · Doctrines bind everything the Watch touches.** The 14 doctrines bind every part of the Watch and everything it touches. *Rationale:* fixes doctrinal scope. *Canon 8 §4.*
- **C8-R-022 · Contradiction is corrected against doctrine.** A clause anywhere in Canon 8 that contradicts a doctrine is defective and is corrected against the doctrine, never the reverse. *Rationale:* doctrine trumps clause. *Canon 8 §4.*

### §4.1 W1 — Eyes never act; hand never judges

- **C8-R-023 · Two engines with one verdict interface.** Detection asserts facts; response executes policy; one verdict is the only interface between them. *Rationale:* enforces engine separation. *Canon 8 §4.1 W1.*
- **C8-R-024 · Detection knowing severance corrupts thresholds.** A detector that knows a severance follows its verdict is tuned defensively — detection quality silently degrades to protect response comfort. *Rationale:* named degradation pattern. *Canon 8 §4.1 W1.*
- **C8-R-025 · Macho holds no powers; Mkono holds no judgement.** Macho is the eyes (senses/asserts/holds no powers); Mkono is the hand (deterministic mapping from verdict properties to actions, no judgement). *Rationale:* fixes engine roles. *Canon 8 §4.1 W1.*
- **C8-R-026 · Learning may detect, never decide (platform rule applied to security).** The platform's own standing rule — learning may detect, it may never decide — applies to security whole. *Rationale:* extends platform rule to Watch. *Canon 8 §4.1 W1.*

### §4.2 W2 — Certainty never exceeds evidence

- **C8-R-027 · Structural certainty only from unfabricatable signals.** Structural certainty is assertable only from signals an outsider cannot fabricate. *Rationale:* fixes structural-certainty basis. *Canon 8 §4.2 W2.*
- **C8-R-028 · Externally steerable caps at reversible containment.** Everything externally steerable caps at reversible containment, whatever its score. *Rationale:* score cap for external signals. *Canon 8 §4.2 W2.*
- **C8-R-029 · Closed sever-certainty registry (six members).** The closed sever-certainty registry — canary touch, attestation failure, mark hit, witness-write attempt, floor violation, mirage incrimination — is the whole of what may unlock maximum autonomous force. *Rationale:* fixes the six members. *Canon 8 §4.2 W2.*
- **C8-R-030 · Certainty function has no confidence parameter.** The certainty computation is a function with no parameter through which a detector's confidence can enter — Solva's assertion boundary transplanted: the signature is the guard. *Rationale:* structural signature enforcement. *Canon 8 §4.2 W2 · cross-ref Canon 1 §4.2.*

### §4.3 W3 — Every autonomous act reversible, bounded, witnessed

- **C8-R-031 · TTL, blast budget, atomic peer-witnessed.** A time-to-live on every action; a blast budget on every window; act-and-attest atomically, peer-countersigned. *Rationale:* fixes the three autonomy safeguards. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-032 · Destroy does not exist in the Watch.** Destroy does not exist in the Watch. *Rationale:* removal-of-power invariant. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-033 · TTL converts false positive from harm to delay.** An action that expires unless renewed converts the false positive from a harm into a delay. *Rationale:* fixes TTL benefit shape. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-034 · Blast budget bounds autoimmune attacks.** A budget converts the autoimmune attack — tricking the immune system into suspending business — from an outage into a bounded cost. *Rationale:* fixes budget benefit shape. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-035 · Atomic attestation makes weaponizing impossible to do quietly.** Atomic attestation makes weaponizing the Watch impossible to do quietly, which for a detection system is the property that matters. *Rationale:* fixes attest benefit shape. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-036 · Watch can stop but not erase.** The Watch can stop anything and erase nothing — deletion remains solely the authority act of Canon 3 §27. *Rationale:* deletion authority is not the Watch's. *Canon 8 §4.3 W3 · cross-ref Canon 3 §27.*

### §4.4 W4 — Deployed in force, never in shadow

- **C8-R-037 · Ship with defined success conditions.** Every Watch mechanism ships with defined conditions of success and runs at full authority from first deployment. *Rationale:* in-force from day one. *Canon 8 §4.4 W4.*
- **C8-R-038 · Undefined parameters = spec gap to close.** A mechanism whose parameters are undefined is a specification gap to close, never a reason to run tentatively. *Rationale:* parameters must be ruled. *Canon 8 §4.4 W4.*
- **C8-R-039 · Watching-only mode spends full value for nothing.** A watching-only mode spends the entire value to buy nothing (promise is response speed). *Rationale:* rationale against shadow deployment. *Canon 8 §4.4 W4.*

### §4.5 W5 — One record, two readings

- **C8-R-040 · Watch maintains no account beyond its own events.** The Watch maintains no account of anything but its own events. *Rationale:* fixes Watch record scope. *Canon 8 §4.5 W5.*
- **C8-R-041 · Watch records are Canon 9 matrix classes.** Its records are classes in the platform's one record per Canon 9's matrix. *Rationale:* uniform record model. *Canon 8 §4.5 W5 · cross-ref Canon 9.*
- **C8-R-042 · Mirage is a rendering, record never lies inward.** The deception the mirage shows an attacker is a rendering; the record never lies inward. *Rationale:* fixes truthful record invariant. *Canon 8 §4.5 W5.*
- **C8-R-043 · One set of facts, two readings.** The compliance owner reads Watch records as assurance; the watch officer reads them as defence; auditor and defender can never disagree, because there is only one set of facts. *Rationale:* fixes single-truth invariant. *Canon 8 §4.5 W5.*

### §4.6 W6 — Suspicion refined, not accumulated

- **C8-R-044 · Route probabilistic to conditions that refine.** Probabilistic detection routes to conditions (friction, narrowed envelopes, the mirage) where the actor's own conduct converts suspicion into certainty or innocence. *Rationale:* fixes refinement path. *Canon 8 §4.6 W6.*

- **C8-R-046 · Mirage is the refinery.** The mirage is the refinery — innocent tripwires nothing and releases; attackers cannot help but incriminate because advancing actions are convicting actions. *Rationale:* fixes mirage function as refiner. *Canon 8 §4.6 W6.*

### §4.7 W7 — Conformance grants trust; non-conformance never convicts

- **C8-R-047 · Signature buys legitimate invisibility.** The behavioural signature buys legitimate actors invisibility. *Rationale:* fixes signature reward. *Canon 8 §4.7 W7.*
- **C8-R-048 · Losing conformance returns to baseline verification only.** Losing conformance returns an actor to baseline verification and nothing more. *Rationale:* forbids sole grounds for conviction. *Canon 8 §4.7 W7.*
- **C8-R-049 · Deviation never sole grounds past friction.** Deviation combines with other signals and is never sole grounds past friction. *Rationale:* fixes conviction floor. *Canon 8 §4.7 W7.*
- **C8-R-050 · Worst outcome to innocent is ordinary day.** The worst the signature can ever do to an innocent is make their day ordinary; this asymmetry is constitutional, not tunable. *Rationale:* fixes constitutional cap. *Canon 8 §4.7 W7.*

### §4.8 W8 — The floor never learns

- **C8-R-051 · Floor is deterministic; moves only by ceremony.** Beneath every adaptive layer stands the ruled floor — deterministic, moved only by visible, delayed, two-signature ceremony. *Rationale:* fixes floor properties. *Canon 8 §4.8 W8.*
- **C8-R-052 · Ceremony is itself a monitored tripwire.** The ceremony is itself a monitored tripwire. *Rationale:* ceremony is observed. *Canon 8 §4.8 W8.*

- **C8-R-054 · Floor moves in daylight, dual-signature, timed, Watch observing.** An attacker who needs the floor moved must request it in daylight, on a countdown, under two names, with the Watch itself among the observers per Canon 3 §24. *Rationale:* fixes floor-move ceremony. *Canon 8 §4.8 W8 · cross-ref Canon 3 §24.*

### §4.9 W9 — No actor enters undeclared

## PART III · Two Engines, One Verdict

### §5 Macho — The Eyes

- **C8-R-079 · Macho observes/scores/asserts; holds no powers.** Macho observes, scores, and asserts facts; it cannot block, throttle, mark, reroute, or notify — sole output is the verdict. *Rationale:* fixes eyes-only powers. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-080 · Macho is consequence-blind.** Because Macho is consequence-blind, it is judged on one thing only — whether the fact was true. *Rationale:* fixes judgement axis. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-081 · Macho corpora/models/thresholds evolve independently of response rules.** Its corpora, models, and thresholds evolve without a single response rule moving. *Rationale:* fixes independence of evolution. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-082 · Macho sensor · Lango (input seat).** Lango scores every payload bound for a model — prompt, retrieved unit, tool result, memory read, agent instruction — for hostility and provenance at the boundary governor's chokepoint, between de-identification and invocation, per Canon 3 §13.1. Graded verdicts: pass, annotate, hold, block. *Rationale:* fixes Lango location + verdicts. *Canon 8 §5 · cross-ref Canon 3 §13.1.*
- **C8-R-083 · Macho sensor · envelope-as-drift.** Every actor's behavioural envelope (tools, data classes, rhythm, depth) is read as drift; the signature is the negative reading of this. *Rationale:* fixes envelope drift sensor. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-084 · Macho sensor · reconciler.** Realised action trees are reconciled against declared mandates within its latency bound. *Rationale:* fixes intent reconciliation. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-085 · Divergence must walk back to originating instruction.** A divergence verdict that cannot walk back to its originating instruction is an opinion and does not count. *Rationale:* fixes provenance requirement. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-086 · Macho sensor · cohort.** Individual conduct is reconciled against the population's compliance norm. *Rationale:* fixes cohort sensor. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-087 · Macho sensor · collision detector.** Signatures are effectively unique at full dimensionality; one signature in two divergent embodiments = hijack; a "new" actor resolving onto a marked signature = re-entry. *Rationale:* fixes collision detection. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-088 · Macho sensor · tripwires.** Planted records, decoy credentials, dyed data — indistinguishable from real to every automated process, reachable by no legitimate path; any touch is structural certainty with zero false positives by construction. *Rationale:* fixes tripwire class. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-089 · Macho sensor · mark reader.** Every gate's mark evaluation is read back as fresh verdicts — the feedback loop that makes a marked actor's every move a new detection. *Rationale:* fixes mark-reader feedback. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-090 · Detection models governed like all learned components.** Detection models are learned and governed like every learned component — calibrated via Canon 4 §49, detection rates measured on uncurated samples drawn from real traffic composition per Canon 1 §4.10. *Rationale:* fixes learned-component discipline. *Canon 8 §5 · cross-ref Canon 4 §49 & Canon 1 §4.10.*
- **C8-R-091 · Engineer inputs; verdict sample untouchable.** Engineering the corpus is craft; touching the verdict sample is a defect. *Rationale:* fixes engineer-vs-sample discipline. *Canon 8 §5.*
- **C8-R-092 · Stale detector renders uncalibrated, loses structural-certainty rights.** A stale detector renders uncalibrated and loses its structural-certainty rights until refreshed. *Rationale:* fixes staleness effect. *Canon 8 §5.*

### §6 The Verdict

- **C8-R-093 · One verdict is the only Macho/Mkono interface.** The two engines meet at exactly one interface: a contract-grade verdict row, externally versioned and closed like refusal registries, signed and replayable like the trust receipt. *Rationale:* single-seam contract. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-094 · Verdict field · identity · detector · pinned version.** Verdict carries verdict identity, detector, pinned version enabling replayability (same record + same version → same verdict). *Rationale:* fixes replay predicate. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-095 · Verdict field · actor · trace reference.** Verdict carries actor and the walkable causal chain to the originating instruction. *Rationale:* fixes provenance chain. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-096 · Verdict field · signal class from closed registry.** Signal class comes from a closed signal registry; additions are registry version bumps, never ad-hoc classes. *Rationale:* closed grammar. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-097 · Verdict field · certainty class (structural/high/probabilistic).** Certainty class is one of structural, high, or probabilistic — asserted by W2 construction rules with steerability pinned per class in the registry, never judged per event. *Rationale:* fixes certainty class + per-class steerability. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-098 · Verdict field · score · window · evidence references.** Verdict carries score, window, and evidence references, every reference resolving into the record. *Rationale:* fixes replayable substance. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-099 · Mkono never reaches behind verdict seam.** Everything the hand needs is in the verdict; Mkono never reaches behind the seam to inspect raw behaviour. *Rationale:* enforces engine independence. *Canon 8 §6.*
- **C8-R-100 · Compromised detector cap = containment with expiry.** A lie in a steerable signal class can summon at most a containment with an expiry. *Rationale:* fixes worst-case blast from corrupted eye. *Canon 8 §6.*

### §7 Mkono — The Hand

- **C8-R-101 · Mkono executes signed policy against verdicts.** Mkono executes signed policy tables against verdicts; it contains no model, no content parsing, no reasoning surface — a deterministic mapping small enough to verify exhaustively. *Rationale:* fixes Mkono properties. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-102 · Policy tables are Registries-class governed values.** Policy tables are Registries-class governed values under Canon 3 §22, authored by the Watch, changed only through ceremony, every table version signed and every action citing the row that fired. *Rationale:* fixes policy governance. *Canon 8 §7 · cross-ref Canon 3 §22.*

- **C8-R-104 · Destroy does not exist in the Watch (restated).** There is no fifth class: destroy does not exist in the Watch, per W3. *Rationale:* re-anchors closed action set. *Canon 8 §7 · cross-ref §4.3 W3.*
- **C8-R-105 · Structural certainty → sever, and hold.** Detection posture Structural certainty → sever and hold; alarm sounds in parallel — people are informed while the machine acts, never consulted before it. *Rationale:* fixes ladder rung 1. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-106 · High/steerable → contain with expiry or the mirage.** Detection posture High/steerable → contain with expiry, or the mirage. *Rationale:* fixes ladder rung 2. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-107 · Mirage entry threshold above containment threshold.** The mirage entry threshold sits above the containment threshold, because deceiving an innocent is a worse harm than throttling one. *Rationale:* fixes mirage ordering. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-108 · Probabilistic → shape only with expiry.** Detection posture Probabilistic → shape only, with expiry. *Rationale:* fixes ladder rung 3. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-109 · Ambiguous/first-seen → default-restrictive envelope.** Detection posture Ambiguous/first-seen → default-restrictive envelope. *Rationale:* fixes ladder rung 4. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-110 · Unknown never = unconstrained AND never = punished.** Unknown never means unconstrained — and never means punished either. *Rationale:* fixes unknown-actor discipline. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-111 · Blast budget ceiling.** Autonomous action may touch at most its ruled share of active actors per rolling window; beyond it, further actions queue for human release. *Rationale:* fixes blast-budget cap. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-112 · Friction budget ceiling.** Share of legitimate actions experiencing any Watch friction has a hard ceiling. *Rationale:* fixes friction-budget cap. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-113 · Hysteresis discipline.** A released actor carries a cooldown before re-action on the same signal class, with escalating dwell; the contain-release-contain pattern is itself a reportable anomaly. *Rationale:* fixes hysteresis + pattern detection. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-114 · Jittered bands, dual-control changes.** Exact trip points live in governed bands, dual-control to change, never published, jittered per evaluation. *Rationale:* fixes threshold-surfer defence. *Canon 8 §7.*
- **C8-R-115 · Redefinition over throughput (Canon 1 §4.7).** A decision class that repeatedly reaches the human panel is a policy defect to redefine, never a queue to work faster. *Rationale:* fixes queue-vs-redefine rule. *Canon 8 §7 · cross-ref Canon 1 §4.7.*

## PART IV · The Strata

### §8 Four Layers of Constraint
