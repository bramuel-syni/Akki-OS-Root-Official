## Part I · Register and Scope

- **C6-R-001 · Journey is not a permission grant.** What a person may actually do is specified in Docs 1, 3, 7; what follows here is what doing it feels like/looks like. *Rationale:* separation of experience from authority. *Canon 6 §1.*
- **C6-R-002 · Plain-language register used throughout Doc 6.** Describes lived experience, not authority; matches Doc 5's screen register. *Canon 6 §2.*
- **C6-R-003 · Role register.** Master Admin ↔ instance owner (Docs 1, 3) · Data Protection Officer ↔ compliance owner (Docs 1, 3, 7) · Operator ↔ operator (Docs 1, 3, 4) · Watch Officer ↔ watch officer (Docs 8, 9). *Canon 6 §2.*
- **C6-R-004 · Business user / product owner / commissioner / technical lead / integrating engineer are motions, not permissions.** A single person may hold several across a week; each motion has one home surface answering its question completely. *Canon 6 §2.*

## Part II · Founding

- **C6-R-005 · Setup — done once, together by instance owner + compliance owner.** Produces an instance running under organisation's own policy with verified sources attached. *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-006 · Setup sequence captures identity + contact + six governed seam values.** Each explained in one plain sentence with recommended default. *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-007 · Setup captures source list with rights held per source.** *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-008 · Setup review step states choices are permanently recorded.** Changing any later requires two approvals. *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-009 · Setup captures first declarations of each seat-holder's role + working interests.** Entered as admission baseline Doc 3 §6.1 requires of every actor thereafter; from that morning no actor enters undeclared; honest change is by re-declaring, not drifting. *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-010 · Source-by-source connect / test / (structured) confirm mapping sample.** Extraction Console Registry Administration screen (Doc 5 Part IV); walked once per source. *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-011 · Setup ends with signed setup record, not config file.** On revisit, screen is read-only; change routes to rule-change ceremony (Doc 3). *Canon 6 §3.*
- **C6-R-012 · Day Zero — first week, instance + compliance owner.** Produces estate map as first deliverable in its own right + signed commissioning certificate. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-013 · Registry Dashboard fills for first time on census.** (Doc 5 §12.1) Holdings per source with proof links · composition by language/era/type/condition with real counts · territory not-yet-measured drawn as such rather than blank · capability estate doesn't need shown unlit not hidden. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-014 · Estate map stands as deliverable independently of anything mined.** Exportable and citable on its own. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-015 · Compliance owner opens Verification Runner in parallel.** Executes plain-language test packs (Doc 3 §31): PII masked before model calls · deletion requires two approvers · unanswerable question refused with reason. Signs commissioning record on demonstrated firing. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-016 · Watch commissions the same way.** Response rails fire in demo: seeded hostile input refused at boundary · planted credential touched + severed · containment expiring clean. Watch officer signs on drill receipts. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-017 · A rail that cannot be demonstrated firing is not in force.** For security exactly as for governance. *Canon 6 §4.*
- **C6-R-018 · Day-zero mining is verification, not value production.** Never a demonstration staged for vendor's benefit; both dashboards live and populated simultaneously = what day zero actually means. *Canon 6 §4.*

## Part III · The Working Rhythm

- **C6-R-019 · Exploration is continuous by business users.** Produces defensible answers + a shortlist of what is worth commissioning next. *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-020 · Registry browsing shows what's extracted + what's not + gap frequency.** *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-021 · Opportunity Briefs (Doc 5 §19) propose what estate could support.** Each figure linked to its measurement, refreshed as census grows, marked advisory throughout. *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-022 · Answer card structure.** Finding in prose · each claim tagged measured/modelled · evidence strip naming sources + strata + privacy-floor confirmation · honesty strip naming what answer cannot say and why · actions (break down, draft memo, walk proof). *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-023 · One action files gap as candidate with own demand evidence.** Enters loop specified in Part VII. *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-024 · Entitlement refusal is a different message from answer card.** Nothing missing from estate, only withheld from this particular asker. *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-025 · Exploration is pre-purchase.** User finds what estate would support before committing budget; Sampling (Doc 5 §16) exists as first-class capability. *Canon 6 §5.*
- **C6-R-026 · Commissioning starts with business user/product owner, then operator.** Produces commissioned objective with accepted quote (priced work). *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-027 · Objective wizard five steps.** Need in plain words → scope from registry-existing material → standards + rights confirmed against explained defaults → plan preview (volume range, reusable stock, expected coverage, what cannot be covered — all before commitment) → commission with confirmation of what becomes permanent. Doc 5 §14. *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-028 · Priced-work quote fields.** Line items · validity window with expiry · delivery estimate as range · cancellation terms with itemised liability for completed work at cancel time. *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-029 · Acceptance is recorded and becomes objective of record.** This is the Freeze (Doc 5 §15) and the immutable event Doc 3 Admit performs underneath. *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-030 · Operator approves via commit review or returns with reason.** Both are ledger events. *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-031 · No-surprises design intent.** Cost + coverage + limits visible before money moves. *Canon 6 §6.*
- **C6-R-032 · Mining is ongoing, not launch event.** Operator + commissioner continuous. Produces finished products with paperwork. *Canon 6 §7.*
- **C6-R-033 · Objective tracking shows coverage-toward-goal with figure basis.** Completed runs each carry proof link; batch board shows quarantined batch with status + resolution path (Doc 4 §31), never simply disappearing. *Canon 6 §7.*
- **C6-R-034 · Deliverables carry contents + licence label + privacy attestation + quality card + proof trail.** *Canon 6 §7.*
- **C6-R-035 · Where rules require, a person releases deliverable through review before recipient.** *Canon 6 §7.*
- **C6-R-036 · Mining is a practice, not a programme with launch date.** *Canon 6 §7.*

## Part IV · Ownership

- **C6-R-038 · Model card carries base model + full training lineage + inherited rights + six-criterion scorecard + version history.** Doc 4 §45 Model Output Acceptance Criteria. *Canon 6 §8.*

## Part V · Assurance

- **C6-R-042 · Assurance is continuous by compliance owner.** Produced by them personally, not received on trust from vendor. *Canon 6 §9.*
- **C6-R-043 · Trust Center content.** Every rule with setting + who set + when + count of automated checks enforcing it; refusals with reasons; quarantines with resolutions; deletions with certificates; rule changes with ceremony stage + countdown; destruction attestations per run; per-application memory activity; quality layer scorecard including honest stale-calibration reporting. *Canon 6 §9.*
- **C6-R-044 · Two Trust Center actions: prove any operation end-to-end + export regulator pack.** Both generated entirely from record (Doc 3 Part VII), never assembled by hand. *Canon 6 §9.*
- **C6-R-045 · Watch Day journey by watch officer, continuously.** Produces threats detected, contained, converted into record's intelligence with legitimate work never interrupted. *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-046 · Watch console state band.** Two latency figures vs bounds · population + trust standing · spend gauges · last drill. Most days first lane (noted, no action) is the whole story. *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-047 · Restraint record is where slow play first becomes a shape.** *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-048 · Watch officer's reserved action set.** Promote containment · extend expiry · sign de-mark · resolve authority disagreement · cancel ceremony in countdown. Nothing else. *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-049 · No threshold visible to tune defensively; no profile readable to browse; no action requires officer before it executes within bounds.** *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-050 · Item repeatedly reaching officer → redefined at policy table, never worked faster.** *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-051 · Drill record is the proof.** Watch proves itself on schedule; proof is receipts, not attestation. *Canon 6 §9-A.*
- **C6-R-052 · Watch journey runs under Watch identity on the shared record.** Per Doc 9 matrix. Compliance owner reads same record through Trust Center under governance identity — same facts, two readings; neither console renders the other's interior. *Canon 6 §9-A.*

## Part VI · The External Path

- **C6-R-053 · Buyer + customer paths specified in Doc 7 Parts V–VI, not restated here.** *Canon 6 §10.*
- **C6-R-054 · Shape of both journeys: know what you're buying + acquire lawfully + receive + integrate + see consumption — all without touching a console or extraction machinery.** *Canon 6 §10.*
- **C6-R-055 · Buyer + customer never hold privileged path.** Both reach platform through identical governed boundary (Doc 5 §28), never varied for commercial relationship however large. *Canon 6 §10.*

## Part VII · The Loop

- **C6-R-056 · Loop shape (4 beats).** Questions arrive from business → most answered, rest = filed gaps with own demand evidence → gaps accumulate + rank themselves (no maintained priority list) → objectives commissioned against top of list, priced with coverage → extraction changes what next round can be answered from. *Canon 6 §11.*
- **C6-R-057 · Demand discovered rather than forecast.** Organisation learns estate value by observing what people try to ask — more reliable than any upfront value assessment. *Canon 6 §11.*
- **C6-R-058 · Loop is self-limiting in a healthy way.** Gap list stops producing high-demand candidates → estate mined to depth its current questions require; mining further = building inventory nobody asked for. *Canon 6 §11.*
- **C6-R-059 · No role required present for loop to continue.** Practice survives staff changes; record carries context (why gap filed, what was tried, what material couldn't yet support); next person doesn't start from nothing. *Canon 6 §11.*
- **C6-R-060 · Every journey rests on identical five things (§12).** (i) One record — same append-only ledger (Doc 3) whichever console; (ii) One boundary — every application any journey touches crosses identical governed boundary (Docs 5, 7), no journey ever finds shorter path; (iii) Three verdicts, never more — answered/held/refused, no fourth invented; (iv) Provenance survives whole path — figure a business user reads traces to same census run a compliance owner would cite; (v) A loop that compounds — what organisation learns in one journey changes what every later journey can accomplish. *Canon 6 §12.*
- **C6-R-061 · Sixth invisible presence: every actor declared/enveloped/watched.** Every gate evaluated the marks; Watch's whole ambition (Doc 8) is legitimate work never noticed it was there. *Canon 6 §12.*
