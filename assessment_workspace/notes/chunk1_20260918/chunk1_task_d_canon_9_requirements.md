## Part I · What This Charter Is

- **C9-R-001 · Two authorities on one substrate.** Governing authority (Docs 2–4) + Watch (Doc 8); both mint + read records; both staff work from shared capacity. *Rationale:* structural framing. *Canon 9 §1.*
- **C9-R-002 · Every cross-authority ambiguity is one of three questions.** Who writes this record · who may read it · under whose identity is this task acting. *Canon 9 §1.*
- **C9-R-003 · Mkondo maintains three registers.** Record matrix · task-identity register · handoff contracts; this charter IS those registers, stated once, cited by both authorities as the same document. *Canon 9 §1.*
- **C9-R-004 · Mkondo governs structure; holds no opinion on content.** Admits nothing, judges nothing, detects nothing, responds to nothing. Both authorities stand on it equally; witness watches both across it. *Canon 9 §1.*
- **C9-R-005 · Question this charter answers is answered here — never by recollection, never differently in two places.** *Canon 9 §1.*
- **C9-R-006 · One-record law precise reading.** D1 §4.3 forbids a second competing account of same events; requires exactly one authoritative account per record class with single writing authority and named readers. Watch minting its own events = law working; two accounts of anyone's events = law broken. X and Y read the same record; X and Z mint new classes; nobody mints a rival. *Canon 9 §2.*

## Part II · The Record Matrix

- **C9-R-007 · Matrix is authoritative.** Every record class + single writer + readers; second writer to any class = defect on sight; unlisted reader does not read; additions are ceremony; matrix version in force stamped on every audit answer. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-008 · Record class · Run ledger rows.** Writes: Northena. Reads: both authorities + compliance surfaces. Admission/gating/convergence/absorbed stamp-audits; contract-grade per D3 §9. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-009 · Record class · Trust receipts.** Writes: SyniSense. Reads: both authorities + receipt's own consumer. Per invocation, tenant-keyed signature. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-010 · Record class · Census and registry records.** Writes: Mtafiti. Reads: both authorities + every surface by proof link. Objective-blind measurement; Watch reads sensitivity classes for tripwire coverage and never writes. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-011 · Record class · Mining plans.** Writes: Targeta. Reads: governing authority + auditors. Watch neither reads to steer nor writes. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-012 · Record class · Reasoning traces · intent audits.** Writes: Solva. Reads: both authorities. Intent-audit divergence fields are emissions Watch reads as signals. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-013 · Record class · Declarations.** Writes: Admission (Northena), from actor. Reads: both authorities. Frozen per D3 §6.1; re-declaration supersedes by new entry. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-014 · Record class · Rules / registries / ceremonies.** Writes: rules service. Reads: both authorities + both consoles. Content authored by owning authority — governance rules by governance, Watch tables by Watch — ceremony administered here for both. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-015 · Record class · Verdicts.** Writes: Macho. Reads: Mkono + both consoles + auditors. The seam artifact of D8 §6; Macho writes nothing else. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-016 · Record class · Action records.** Writes: Mkono. Reads: both authorities + both consoles. Act-and-attest atomic; policy row cited; expiry state carried. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-017 · Record class · Attestations / drill receipts.** Writes: Watch workers + the witness. Reads: everyone with assurance standing. Break-in-test failures-to-modify land here as measurements. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-018 · Record class · Marks.** Writes: Mkono. Reads: every gate on every pass. Dimension set per D8 §10; de-mark entries carry two signatures. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-019 · Record class · Behavioural profiles.** Writes: signature substrate. Reads: **no surface, ever**. W13: parameters only, tenant-keyed, non-exportable; trust standing derives outward, internals never do. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-020 · Record class · Memory-plane rows.** Writes: owning application via its plane. Reads: estate holder + compliance. Per D7 Part IV; Watch reads plane activity as behaviour, never plane content as data. *Canon 9 §3.*
- **C9-R-021 · Reading discipline.** Reads are as governed as writes. Watch reads governance evidence to protect estate and for no other purpose; governance reads Watch records for assurance and never to re-litigate a verdict; neither authority's surfaces render the other's interior. *Canon 9 §4.*
- **C9-R-022 · Same facts, two readings, by construction.** Compliance owner sees Watch acted + why at record level, never thresholds/placements; watch officer sees run-record facts, never governance's deliberations. *Canon 9 §4.*

## Part III · The Task-Identity Register

- **C9-R-023 · One task, one identity.** Every task class maps to exactly one authority's identity and scope before it executes. *Canon 9 §5.*
- **C9-R-024 · Unmapped task class does not run.** Not runs-with-a-warning; does not run. *Canon 9 §5.*
- **C9-R-025 · Workers serve both authorities across time, never within a task.** Evidence crosses between authorities only through the matrix, provenance-marked with the identity that produced it, never sideways through a dual-hatted task. *Canon 9 §5.*
- **C9-R-026 · Structural enforcement of the register.** No credential scope exists that spans both authorities — defect D8 (D7 §47) is unrepresentable rather than policed. *Canon 9 §5.*
- **C9-R-027 · Task-identity mapping table.** Admission/gating/convergence/ledger writes → Governance · Northena (includes declaration capture at admit). Custody chain/chokepoint/egress → Governance · SyniSense. Census/measurement/targeting/reasoning → Governance · owning engine (per Doc 4). Rule ceremony administration → Governance · rules service (for both authorities' values; content authorship stays with owner). Sensing/scoring/reconciliation/verdict minting → Watch · Macho (read-only against governance records). Response execution/marks/mirage operation/trust discounts → Watch · Mkono (writes only its own classes; no write to any governance class). Attestation/watchdog/break-in test → the witness (neither authority's credentials; alarms outward). Console sessions → the console's authority (Trust Center under governance, Watch Console under Watch; each session receipted under own identity). *Canon 9 §5.*
- **C9-R-028 · Input-seat two-task pattern.** The input seat's sensing runs under Watch identity at the same physical point as the SyniSense custody chain — two tasks, two identities, one location. *Canon 9 §5.*

## Part IV · The Handoff Contracts

- **C9-R-029 · Every crossing is a registered contract.** What crosses, in what shape, under whose identity, with what receipt. Unregistered handoff is a finding. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-030 · Handoff · Admission → extraction.** Frozen artifact + declaration cross whole; D3 §5–§8 and D4 §34.2 govern; receipt is the admit row. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-031 · Handoff · Chokepoint → model.** Custody chain per D3 §13.1 with input-seat verdict preceding invocation; hold here renders through refusal taxonomy; receipt is trust receipt + verdict. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-032 · Handoff · Verdict → response.** Seam of D8 §6: verdict row in, action out, policy version cited; no other channel between the engines exists. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-033 · Handoff · Containment → decision.** Reserved-decision item crosses to Decisions panel with walk attached; resolution crosses back as ceremony-grade entry; expiry governs the interim. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-034 · Handoff · Mirage release → reconciliation.** Release verdict crosses to replay-or-notify duty under ruled posture; discharge receipted; friction budget debited. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-035 · Handoff · Disagreement → resolution.** Both authorities' records cross side by side; human's resolution lands in both readings as one entry; neither record is amended — corrected by new entry only. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-036 · Handoff · Watch findings → CIF.** Findings cross as detection input under CIF's own origination port; nothing crosses back but ratified frames through the coach seat. *Canon 9 §6.*
- **C9-R-037 · Charter changes by rule-change ceremony.** Proposed → counter-signed → waited → applied → re-verified; matrix changes carry slowest countdown class because matrix is the boundary of both authorities at once. *Canon 9 §7.*
- **C9-R-038 · Precedence.** Where this charter and any other document appear to disagree about who writes/reads/is-acting, this charter governs; where silent, owning authority's canon governs; silence that mattered = finding to close by amendment, never gap to fill by recollection. *Canon 9 §7.*
- **C9-R-039 · One-line essence.** One account per record class · one identity per task · one contract per crossing — held in one short document both authorities cite. Seamlessness comes from reads; boundaries come from writes; whole point of writing it down is that nobody ever answers a matrix question from memory. *Canon 9 §8.*
