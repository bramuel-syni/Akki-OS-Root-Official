# Canon 2 — The Conditioned Ideation Faculty (CIF) — Extracted Requirements

## Part I — The Promise

- **C2-001 (§1)**: CIF governs how the system's own judgements are made, retained, and improved. Peer to Northena/SyniSense/Solva/Mtafiti/Targeta, subordinate to none.
- **C2-002 (§1)**: CIF operates across the build loop, the training path, planning, and proposal generation — wherever the system or its builders render a judgement.
- **C2-003 (§1)**: CIF is in force from adoption. No phasing, no activation sequence. Every operation in every engine executes under this specification from its first occurrence.
- **C2-004 (§3 Four-part promise, held to a measure)**: 
    (a) Repeated corrections are eliminated — a correction retires a *class* of error, not the instance.
    (b) Shown confidence has visible footing.
    (c) Rejected/deferred ideas wait alive with named conditions and return when conditions arrive.
    (d) A reframe rewrites everything the old thinking touched in one strike.
- **C2-005 (§3)**: Measure of success: the human corrects LESS each cycle for the same or better output. That is the adoption claim. Stronger claims only on cycles where measured.

## Part II — Governing Principle

- **C2-006 (§5 Single-experiment principle)**: The assembled system is the sole object under test. Nothing beneath is experimental. Every mechanic is known and parameterisable, deploys in force with conditions of success strictly implemented and quality measured — or its parameters are undefined (a spec gap to close), NEVER pilot/shadow/observe-first.
- **C2-007 (§5)**: No capability is gated on demonstration/observation/trial/seeing-first. Gates bind spend, output quality, or a claim — never existence or force.
- **C2-008 (§5)**: CIF enters under this principle without qualification. Its mechanics are proven realities, not a pilot. Construction order carries no epistemic weight.

## Part II — Referenced Systems (§6)

- **C2-009 (§6 The coach)**: The coach (human role — teacher, auditor, informer) is constitutionally load-bearing. No configuration of the system operates without this seat filled. Only the coach may adopt a new frame.
- **C2-010 (§6 Solva-pattern validation)**: CIF's own claims are evaluated by Solva's reasoning discipline (claims stated at their evidence class, support meets the bar, contradiction surfaced, refusal names reason and what would satisfy).
- **C2-011 (§6 The Critic Seam)**: The standing quality layer for worker output (Doc 7). CIF's pre-ship checklist extends the Critic Seam rubric.
- **C2-012 (§6 Independence rule)**: A reviewing instance must differ genuinely from what it reviews — different base model or materially different context shape. Same-base same-context review shares blind spots.
- **C2-013 (§6 Run telemetry)**: CIF's monitoring parameters bind to named fields in the platform's per-run telemetry.
- **C2-014 (§6 The Watch)**: Watch findings are a source CIF may metabolise like any other ground reality. Drills operate under CIF's own permanent bound — Watch never certifies its own framing; fitness counted in demonstrated firings, never self-graded value.
- **C2-015 (§6 Engine walls)**: A planner's learning never widens what an objective may reach; a critic detects and never decides. CIF operates inside these walls everywhere.

## Part IV — Core Practice

- **C2-016 (§8 Manifests)**: Every shipped verdict and every idea pick carries its manifest. An unmanifested verdict is a DEFECT.
- **C2-017 (§8)**: Manifest entries are evidence-classed: a verdict resting on inferred entries states its provisionality where it ships, not in a footnote.
- **C2-018 (§8)**: A selection records WHY the winner ranked first — the pick carries its own manifest.
- **C2-019 (§8)**: Silence is a data state, never confirmation. A response receiving no verdict from a human is archived as unverified-provisional, NOT counted as agreed.
- **C2-020 (§9 Living archive)**: Every evaluated idea lands in an archive state THE SAME CYCLE. Nothing exits silently. Four states: adopted / not-yet-conditioned (missing condition named) / unverified-provisional / superseded.
- **C2-021 (§9)**: A rejection for circumstantial reasons names the missing condition specifically enough that its arrival is recognisable — not "not ready" but WHAT precisely would make it ready.
- **C2-022 (§9)**: Every archive entry carries rent: a review-by date and a re-qualification budget. Expired entries are re-qualified or superseded, never left to sit.
- **C2-023 (§9)**: A resurfaced idea re-enters with ZERO inherited credibility. New candidate, fully re-qualified against the current goal.
- **C2-024 (§9)**: Re-qualification is paper-first. Re-scoring under changed assumptions precedes compute; simulation runs only where the paper verdict warrants.

## Part IV — Selection Quality

- **C2-025 (§10.1 The naivety pass)**: On demand or when a workstream stagnates, candidates may be re-qualified with inherited constraints struck ("known not to work", "nobody does it this way", "too simple to matter") — GROUND-TRUTH constraints (law, physics, custody, budget) remain untouched.
- **C2-026 (§10.1)**: Every struck constraint receives a short evidence brief answering why consensus holds it, presented for a HUMAN call. The pass never classifies autonomously; a survivor never ships without its autopsy attached.
- **C2-027 (§10.2 Pre-ship checklist)**: The coach's correction history is maintained as a readable checklist of known failure classes, applied to picks and verdicts before they ship.
- **C2-028 (§10.2)**: The checklist is REPLAY-ONLY: applies known classes, never claims novel coverage. Enters the Critic Seam's standing rubric as an amendment, not a parallel mechanism.

## Part V — The Frame Lifecycle

- **C2-029 (§11 Detection)**: Three frame signatures watched continuously from the manifest corpus: (a) cascade — one strike flips many verdicts at once; (b) absorption — same correction class patched repeatedly while pattern persists; (c) misfit — information reduces to no existing structure, contradicts standing positions, or resists the manifest probe. An observed signature escalates as a frame-candidate finding IN THE SAME CYCLE it was observed.
- **C2-030 (§11)**: The faculty DETECTS. It NEVER adopts a frame on the strength of its own signature.
- **C2-031 (§12 Origination)**: A new frame enters ONLY through the origination port: the coach, ground reality observed in deployment, or a genuinely outside reviewer. This seat is constitutionally load-bearing and permanent in every configuration.
- **C2-032 (§13 Metabolisation)**: When a frame is adopted, four things follow IN ORDER, none optional: 
    (1) Adoption record names the overturned structure, not merely the new statement.
    (2) Every archived verdict whose manifest touches the overturned structure re-qualifies on paper. Sweep completes before any new verdict issues on the affected class.
    (3) Adopted frame enters the manifest vocabulary — every subsequent verdict declares its position relative to it.
    (4) Closure holds when the absorption signature for that correction class has been quiet for three cycles.
- **C2-033 (§13)**: A frame-adoption commit WITHOUT its sweep record FAILS its own build check — the same discipline as a function landing with no registry row.

## Part VI — Monitoring (§14)

- **C2-034 (§14 Condition watchers)**: Automated monitoring of an archived idea's named conditions, bound only to conditions that resolve to named live telemetry fields. An unbindable condition carries a rent-date human review instead. A fired watcher re-queues its idea as a candidate; it NEVER adopts on its own.
- **C2-035 (§14 Cascade detection)**: Multi-flip identification across the manifest corpus, escalating as a frame-candidate finding under the same rule as any other detected signature.
- **C2-036 (§14 Adversarial manifest elicitation)**: Second-instance interrogation of shipped verdicts, satisfying the independence rule — different reviewer, never same-base echo.

## Part VII — Permanent Bound

- **C2-037 (§15)**: NO parameter set exists whose truth would license the faculty to validate its own framing. Self-certification is closed by construction.
- **C2-038 (§15)**: A proposal for a self-certification path is an EXHIBIT of the exact failure this bound prevents — rejected on sight, not debated on merits.
- **C2-039 (§15)**: The self-certification function is instead held by three things acting together: the faculty detects and metabolises; the coach originates and ratifies; Solva-pattern validation keeps evidence honest. The loop closes THROUGH the coach seat, never AROUND it.

## Part VIII — Operative Parameters (§16 — FACT-class)

Held every cycle:
- **C2-040**: Manifest coverage — 100% of shipped verdicts and picks carry a manifest.
- **C2-041**: Archive completeness — every evaluated idea lands in a state the same cycle.
- **C2-042**: Escalation latency — a frame signature escalates in the cycle it is observed.
- **C2-043**: Metabolisation execution — four-step sweep triggers within one cycle of adoption; completes before new verdicts issue on the affected class.
- **C2-044**: Rent collection — archive review-by dates honoured; expired entries re-qualified or superseded, never left standing.

## Part VIII — Quality Parameters (§17 — measured)

- **C2-045 (§17 Promise metric)**: Owner-confirmed repeat-class corrections decline across cycles. Flat/rising for 2 consecutive cycles → triggers CIF review.
- **C2-046 (§17 Manifest hit-rate)**: When a flip occurs, struck assumption was in the manifest ≥ 70% DEFAULT. Below → repair elicitation protocol.
- **C2-047 (§17 Provisional honesty)**: ZERO instances of inferred-footing content shipped into fact register. One instance = finding on record, not a rounding error.
- **C2-048 (§17 Metabolisation closure)**: Absorption signature for an adopted frame is quiet within 3 cycles DEFAULT. Noisy → metabolisation ruled incomplete; sweep re-runs.
- **C2-049 (§17 Overhead ceiling)**: CIF practice consumes no more than an owner-set share of cycle budget. Breach shrinks CIF; product work never absorbs it.
- **C2-050 (§17 False-escalation rate)**: Frame candidates later ruled non-events ≤ 30% DEFAULT. Above → detection thresholds tighten.
- **C2-051 (§17)**: DEFAULT-class values are cheap to correct, revised on evidence at a cycle boundary, never mid-cycle, and never requiring this specification to be reopened.

## Part IX — Enforcement (§18)

- **C2-052 (§18)**: CIF uses the platform's standing enforcement vehicles. It introduces NO new ones.
- **C2-053 (§18 Manifests)**: Schema-required fields on every verdict-bearing artifact; unmanifested verdict rejected at submission (standing format-gate).
- **C2-054 (§18 Archive)**: Entries are append-only ledger rows. A standing query surfaces any evaluated-but-unarchived idea as a finding.
- **C2-055 (§18 Evidence classing)**: CIF's own claims evaluated by the assertion boundary exactly as any answer — no separate leniency.
- **C2-056 (§18 Metabolisation)**: Frame-adoption commit lacking its sweep record fails its build check.
- **C2-057 (§18 Rent/monitoring)**: Review dates and watcher precision are queryable fields; expiries and breaches surface through the same standing-query machinery.
- **C2-058 (§18 Selection defects)**: Enter the Critic Seam's rubric as a named amendment class — not a parallel checklist.
- **C2-059 (§18)**: Live conversational reasoning (a judgement spoken before it becomes an artifact) is governed as behavioural discipline with a mechanical audit backstop, NOT intercepted mid-thought. Everything that lands is gated; the ephemeral layer is audited, not policed in flight.

## Part X — Governance (§19–§20)

- **C2-060 (§19)**: CIF's yield is counted in owner-confirmed catches only. Self-graded value is INADMISSIBLE.
- **C2-061 (§19)**: No CIF output may claim coach-independence, in any configuration, permanently. Origination port is load-bearing every time.
- **C2-062 (§19)**: CIF is entry #1 in its own archive: manifested, rented, revisable under exactly the rules it applies to everything else.
- **C2-063 (§19)**: Paper precedes compute; detection never decides; engine walls hold everywhere CIF operates — no exception for the faculty itself.
- **C2-064 (§20 What CIF does not do)**: Does not originate frames, adjudicate its own framing, or replace the coach seat.
- **C2-065 (§20)**: Does not block work, hold veto authority, or acquire a review layer above itself. There is no meta-CIF; proposing one is itself a frame-absorption finding.
- **C2-066 (§20)**: Does not promise a zero residual. The assumption never recorded is the one that flips unseen — value is stated as HALVING the residual, never eliminating it.
