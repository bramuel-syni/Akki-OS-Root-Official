# Canon 6 — Akki User Journeys — Extracted Requirements

Source: `/app/assessment_workspace/canon/text/Akki_Canon_6_User_Journeys.txt`
(extracted from `Akki_Canon_6_User_Journeys.docx`). Row IDs `C6-###`.

Doc 6 is a journey document — it describes what doing platform work *feels
like* rather than granting permissions. The concrete requirements below are
the behaviours the journeys make binding by describing them as reality.

## Part I — How to Read / Role Register

- **C6-001 (§1)**: A journey is NOT a permission grant. What a person may actually do is specified in Docs 1, 3, 7. This document describes what doing it feels like and looks like.
- **C6-002 (§1)**: Where this document and Doc 3 appear to say different things, Doc 3's authority model governs what actually admits or refuses.
- **C6-003 (§2 Role register — on-screen labels)**:
    - Master Admin → addressed on screen as "the instance owner"
    - Data Protection Officer → "the compliance owner"
    - Operator → "the operator" (unchanged in both registers)
    - Watch Officer → "the watch officer"
- **C6-004 (§2 Additional plain-language terms)**: "business user / product owner" (no standing permission beyond any employee); "commissioner" (per commission, not a fixed seat); "technical lead / integrating engineer" (employee or contracted 3rd party).
- **C6-005 (§2)**: These are MOTIONS, not a permissions taxonomy. One person may hold several across a week (the compliance owner asking an exploratory question is also, in that moment, a business user).
- **C6-006 (§2)**: Each motion has ONE home surface answering its question completely — never a console that tries to serve every motion.

## Part II — Founding

### §3 Setup

- **C6-007 (§3)**: Setup performed once by the instance owner + compliance owner TOGETHER.
- **C6-008 (§3)**: Setup captures: (a) organisation identity; (b) compliance owner contact; (c) SIX governed seam values, each explained in one plain sentence with a recommended default; (d) source list with rights held on each.
- **C6-009 (§3)**: The review step states plainly that choices are PERMANENTLY recorded and changing any later requires two approvals.
- **C6-010 (§3)**: Setup also captures first declarations per §6.1 of Doc 3 — role and working interests. From that morning forward no actor (person or agent) enters undeclared. Honest change is by re-declaration, not drift.
- **C6-011 (§3)**: Source connection sequence: connect → test → for structured sources, confirm the mapping sample. Walked ONCE PER SOURCE, never all at once. Implemented on the Extraction Console's Registry Administration screen (Doc 5 Part IV).
- **C6-012 (§3)**: The Setup journey ends with a SIGNED setup record, not a configuration file. On revisit the screen is read-only; a change request routes to the rule-change ceremony (Doc 3).

### §4 Day Zero

- **C6-013 (§4)**: Day Zero is performed by the instance owner + compliance owner in the FIRST WEEK.
- **C6-014 (§4)**: Day Zero deliverables: (a) estate map as a first deliverable in its own right; (b) a signed commissioning certificate.
- **C6-015 (§4)**: The Registry Dashboard (Doc 5 §12.1) fills for the first time: holdings per source with proof links; composition by language/era/type/condition with real counts; unmeasured territory drawn as such (never blank); capability the estate does not need shown UNLIT (never hidden).
- **C6-016 (§4)**: For most organisations Day Zero produces the first complete measured picture of their own data — exportable and citable independently of anything mined later.
- **C6-017 (§4)**: In parallel, compliance owner opens the Verification Runner (Doc 3 §31) and executes plain-language test packs (e.g. personal data masked before any model call; deletion requires two approvers; an unanswerable question is refused with a reason).
- **C6-018 (§4)**: Each pack shows the rail firing WITH ITS PROOF. Compliance owner signs the commissioning record on that basis.
- **C6-019 (§4)**: The Watch commissions the same way: seeded hostile input refused at the boundary; planted credential touched and severed; a containment expiring clean. Watch officer signs on the drill receipts.
- **C6-020 (§4)**: A rail that cannot be demonstrated firing is not in force, for security exactly as for governance.
- **C6-021 (§4)**: Day-zero mining is VERIFICATION that the organisation's own parameters work — NEVER value production, never a demonstration staged for the vendor.
- **C6-022 (§4)**: Both dashboards (Registry and Trust Center) live and populated SIMULTANEOUSLY = Day Zero.

## Part III — Working Rhythm

### §5 Exploration

- **C6-023 (§5)**: A business user browses the registry: what has been extracted and verified, what has not, which gaps others have hit, how often.
- **C6-024 (§5)**: Opportunity Briefs (Doc 5 §19) propose what the estate could support; each figure linked to its measurement; refreshed as census grows; marked advisory throughout.
- **C6-025 (§5)**: The answer arrives as an ANSWER CARD: (a) finding stated in prose; (b) each individual claim tagged measured or modelled; (c) evidence strip naming sources/strata and confirming the privacy floor held; (d) honesty strip naming plainly what the answer cannot say and why; (e) actions — break it down / draft the memo / walk the proof.
- **C6-026 (§5)**: Where the answer meets a genuine gap, ONE action files the work as a candidate carrying its own demand evidence — entering the loop of §11.
- **C6-027 (§5)**: Where the answer meets an entitlement rule, the user sees an ENTITLEMENT refusal in place of the answer card — a different message entirely.
- **C6-028 (§5)**: Exploration is deliberately PRE-PURCHASE — the user finds out what the estate would support before committing budget. This is why Sampling (Doc 5 §16) is a first-class capability.

### §6 Commissioning and Buying

- **C6-029 (§6)**: The objective wizard (Doc 5 §14) turns a need into governed work in five steps:
    (1) the need stated in plain words;
    (2) scope selected from what the registry says actually exists;
    (3) standards and rights confirmed against explained defaults;
    (4) a plan preview — extraction volume as a range, reusable stock already identified, expected coverage of the goal, and WHAT CANNOT BE COVERED, all shown before any commitment;
    (5) commissioning itself, with a confirmation stating precisely what becomes permanent at that moment.
- **C6-030 (§6)**: Where the work is priced, a quote follows: line items, validity window with expiry, delivery estimate as a range, cancellation terms with itemised liability for whatever has already completed.
- **C6-031 (§6)**: Acceptance is recorded and becomes the objective of record (the Freeze of Doc 5 §15 / Doc 3 Admit).
- **C6-032 (§6)**: The operator approves the work to run through commit review, or returns it with a reason. Both are ledger events.
- **C6-033 (§6)**: Journey design intent: no one is EVER surprised — cost, coverage, and limits visible before any money moves.

### §7 Mining and Delivery

- **C6-034 (§7)**: Extraction runs under the frozen objective. Objective tracking shows coverage toward the goal, basis of the figure stated alongside; completed runs each carry a proof link; a batch board shows quarantined batches with status and resolution path (Doc 4 §31).
- **C6-035 (§7)**: Deliverables land on the shelf as they complete: datasets, briefs, standing feeds, occurrence indexes — each carrying its contents, licence label, privacy attestation, quality card, and proof trail.
- **C6-036 (§7)**: Where the instance's own rules require it, a person releases a deliverable through review before it reaches its recipient.
- **C6-037 (§7)**: Mining is ongoing work, not a launch event. An organisation develops a mining practice, does not schedule a programme.

## Part IV — Ownership

### §8 Ownership and Integration

- **C6-038 (§8)**: As extraction accumulates labelled material, adapters train on it.
- **C6-039 (§8)**: Each accepted model appears with: base model; full training lineage and inherited rights; six-criterion acceptance scorecard with real measured numbers (Doc 4 §45 MOAC); complete version history.
- **C6-040 (§8)**: Before any model exists for a given class, the surface says so PLAINLY rather than rendering an empty error.
- **C6-041 (§8)**: Integration is commissioned in ONE flow: the application's standing objective is shaped; its memory plane configured (retrieval scope, write-back on/off with per-cycle volume ceiling, storage ceiling, retention, precompute parameters — Doc 7 §19–23); and a scoped key issued — all as one recorded act.
- **C6-042 (§8)**: The engineer registers a webhook, reads the live answer envelope INCLUDING ALL THREE REFUSAL SHAPES, makes a test call, watches it appear in the compliance record IMMEDIATELY.
- **C6-043 (§8)**: No warm-up period in which an integration runs unobserved — the first call is governed exactly like the thousandth.

## Part V — Assurance

### §9 Trust Center

- **C6-044 (§9)**: The Trust Center holds every rule with its setting, who set it and when, and the count of automated checks actually enforcing it — plus the full track record: refusals with reasons, quarantines with resolutions, deletions with certificates, rule changes with ceremony stage and countdown, destruction attestations per run, per-application memory activity, and the quality layer's own scorecard including when its own calibration has gone stale.
- **C6-045 (§9)**: One action proves any operation end to end; another exports the regulator pack. Both generated entirely from the record (Doc 3 Part VII), never assembled by hand.

### §9-A The Watch Day

- **C6-046 (§9-A)**: Watch officer opens the Watch Console to its state band: two latency figures against bounds, population and trust standing, spend gauges, last drill.
- **C6-047 (§9-A)**: Reading the noted-no-action lane IS the job — the restraint record is where the slow play first becomes a shape.
- **C6-048 (§9-A)**: Watch officer promotes a suspect into misdirection and watches the session as occupancy and findings.
- **C6-049 (§9-A)**: An innocent inside misdirection does ordinary work and releases on expiry, period reconciled honestly. A guilty actor touching a planted credential produces a structural finding: mark minted, severance executes on its OWN authority, and the decision panel receives the aftermath rather than the request.
- **C6-050 (§9-A)**: What the officer NEVER does is as designed as what they do: no threshold visible to tune defensively; no profile readable to browse; no action requires them before it executes within its bounds.
- **C6-051 (§9-A)**: Reserved decisions are exactly: promote a containment; extend an expiry; sign a de-mark; resolve a disagreement between the authorities; cancel a ceremony in countdown.
- **C6-052 (§9-A)**: A class of item that keeps reaching the human panel is redefined at the policy table, never worked faster.
- **C6-053 (§9-A)**: The drill record answers the auditor's question before it is asked — the watch proves itself on schedule, and the proof is RECEIPTS, not attestation.
- **C6-054 (§9-A)**: Watch runs under Watch identity on the shared record per matrix in Doc 9. Compliance owner reads the SAME record through the Trust Center under governance identity — same facts, two readings; neither console renders the other's interior.

## Part VI — The External Path

- **C6-055 (§10)**: Buyer path and customer portal specified in Doc 7 Parts V–VI. Not restated. Shape: know what you are buying → acquire it lawfully → receive it → integrate against it → see exactly what you have consumed, all WITHOUT touching a console or the extraction machinery.
- **C6-056 (§10)**: Neither buyer nor customer holds a privileged path. Both reach the platform through the identical governed boundary (Doc 5 §28), never varied for a commercial relationship however large.

## Part VII — The Loop (§11)

- **C6-057 (§11)**: Loop, four beats:
    Questions arrive from the business → asked of the estate
    ↓
    Most answered — the rest produce filed gaps carrying their own demand evidence
    ↓
    Gaps accumulate and rank themselves — no one maintains a priority list
    ↓
    Objectives commissioned against the top of that list, priced with coverage stated
    ↓
    The resulting extraction changes what the next round of questions can be answered from
- **C6-058 (§11)**: Demand is DISCOVERED, not forecast — the organisation learns its estate's worth by observing what people try to ask.
- **C6-059 (§11)**: Loop self-limits healthily — when gap list stops producing high-demand candidates, the estate has been mined to current questions' depth.
- **C6-060 (§11)**: No role is required to be present for the loop to continue — this survives staff changes. The record carries context that would otherwise live in someone's head.

## Part VII — Common to Every Journey (§12)

- **C6-061 (§12)**: Every journey rests on FIVE things simultaneously (none optional):
    (1) One record — same append-only ledger regardless of console.
    (2) One boundary — every application (first-party or third) crosses the identical governed boundary. No journey finds a shorter path.
    (3) Three verdicts, never more — every step resolves to answered / held / refused. No fourth outcome.
    (4) Provenance that survives the whole path — a figure in Exploration traces WITHOUT exception to the same census run cited in Assurance.
    (5) A loop that compounds — what the organisation learns in one journey changes what every later journey can accomplish.
- **C6-062 (§12)**: Beneath all five: every actor in every journey was DECLARED, ENVELOPED, and WATCHED; every gate evaluated the marks; the Watch's ambition is that people doing legitimate work never notice it was there.
