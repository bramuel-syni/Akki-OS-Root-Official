# Canon 9 — The Mkondo Charter — Extracted Requirements

Source: `/app/assessment_workspace/canon/text/Akki_Canon_9_Mkondo_Charter.txt`
(extracted from `Akki_Canon_9_Mkondo_Charter.docx`). Row IDs `C9-###`.

The Mkondo Charter is the orchestration charter — one place naming, for every
ambiguity between the governing authority and the Watch, WHO writes, WHO reads,
and UNDER WHOSE IDENTITY a task acts.

## Part I — Charter Scope

- **C9-001 (§1)**: Mkondo is the orchestration service maintaining THREE registers (record matrix, task-identity register, handoff contracts) and ENFORCING them. Both authorities (governance and Watch) cite this charter as the same document.
- **C9-002 (§1)**: Mkondo governs STRUCTURE and holds NO OPINION about content. It admits nothing, judges nothing, detects nothing, responds to nothing.
- **C9-003 (§1)**: A question this charter answers is answered HERE — never by recollection, never differently in two places.
- **C9-004 (§2 One-record law, precisely)**: Doc 1 §4.3 holds: exactly one record; nothing maintains a second. What's forbidden: a second competing account of the same events. What's required: ONE authoritative account per record class, with a single writing authority and named readers.
- **C9-005 (§2)**: X and Y read the same record; X and Z mint new classes; NOBODY mints a rival.

## Part II — The Record Matrix (§3)

Every record class → its single writer → its readers. Additions to the matrix
are CEREMONY. A second writer to any class is a defect on sight. A reader not
listed does not read. The matrix VERSION IN FORCE is stamped on every audit answer.

- **C9-006 (§3 Run ledger rows)**: Writer = Northena. Readers = both authorities + compliance surfaces. Records admission, gating, convergence, absorbed stamp-audits. Contract-grade per Doc 3 §9.
- **C9-007 (§3 Trust receipts)**: Writer = SyniSense. Readers = both authorities + the receipt's own consumer. Per invocation, tenant-keyed signature.
- **C9-008 (§3 Census and registry records)**: Writer = Mtafiti. Readers = both authorities + every surface by proof link. Objective-blind measurement; Watch reads sensitivity classes for tripwire coverage but never writes.
- **C9-009 (§3 Mining plans)**: Writer = Targeta. Readers = governing authority + auditors. Watch neither reads to steer nor writes.
- **C9-010 (§3 Reasoning traces / intent audits)**: Writer = Solva. Readers = both authorities. The intent audit's divergence fields are emissions the Watch reads as signals.
- **C9-011 (§3 Declarations)**: Writer = Admission (Northena), from the actor. Readers = both authorities. Frozen per Doc 3 §6.1; re-declaration supersedes by new entry.
- **C9-012 (§3 Rules · registries · ceremonies)**: Writer = the rules service. Readers = both authorities + both consoles. CONTENT authored by the owning authority — governance rules by governance, Watch tables by the Watch — CEREMONY administered here for both.
- **C9-013 (§3 Verdicts)**: Writer = Macho. Readers = Mkono + both consoles + auditors. The seam artefact of Doc 8 §6. Macho writes nothing else.
- **C9-014 (§3 Action records)**: Writer = Mkono. Readers = both authorities + both consoles. Act-and-attest atomic; policy row cited; expiry state carried.
- **C9-015 (§3 Attestations / drill receipts)**: Writer = Watch workers + the witness. Readers = everyone with assurance standing. Break-in test's failures-to-modify land here as measurements.
- **C9-016 (§3 Marks)**: Writer = Mkono. Readers = every gate on every pass. Dimension set per Doc 8 §10. De-mark entries carry two signatures.
- **C9-017 (§3 Behavioural profiles)**: Writer = the signature substrate. Readers = NO surface, ever (W13). Parameters only; tenant-keyed; NON-EXPORTABLE; trust standing derives outward; internals never do.
- **C9-018 (§3 Memory-plane rows)**: Writer = the owning application via its plane. Readers = the estate holder + compliance. Per Doc 7 Part IV. Watch reads plane ACTIVITY as behaviour, never plane CONTENT as data.

## Part II — Reading Discipline (§4)

- **C9-019 (§4)**: Reads are as governed as writes.
- **C9-020 (§4)**: Watch reads governance evidence TO PROTECT THE ESTATE and for no other purpose.
- **C9-021 (§4)**: Governance reads Watch records FOR ASSURANCE and never to re-litigate a verdict.
- **C9-022 (§4)**: Neither authority's surfaces render the OTHER's INTERIOR — compliance owner sees THAT the Watch acted and why at the record level, never thresholds or placements; watch officer sees the run record's facts, never governance's deliberations. SAME FACTS, TWO READINGS.

## Part III — Task-Identity Register (§5)

- **C9-023 (§5)**: Every task class maps to EXACTLY ONE authority's identity and scope BEFORE it executes.
- **C9-024 (§5)**: An unmapped task class DOES NOT RUN — not "runs with a warning"; does not run.
- **C9-025 (§5)**: Workers serve both authorities ACROSS time and never WITHIN a task.
- **C9-026 (§5)**: Evidence crosses between authorities ONLY through the matrix above, provenance-marked with the producing identity — never sideways through a dual-hatted task.
- **C9-027 (§5)**: Enforcement is STRUCTURAL: no credential scope exists that spans both authorities. Defect D8 (Doc 7 §47) is UNREPRESENTABLE rather than policed.

### Task-class → identity map

- **C9-028**: Admission · gating · convergence · ledger writes → Governance · Northena. Includes declaration capture at admit.
- **C9-029**: Custody chain · chokepoint · egress → Governance · SyniSense. Input seat's sensing runs under Watch identity at the same physical point — TWO tasks, TWO identities, one location.
- **C9-030**: Census · measurement · targeting · reasoning → Governance · owning engine (per Doc 4).
- **C9-031**: Rule ceremony administration → Governance · rules service. For BOTH authorities' values; content authorship stays with the owner.
- **C9-032**: Sensing · scoring · reconciliation · verdict minting → Watch · Macho. Read-only against governance records.
- **C9-033**: Response execution · marks · mirage operation · trust discounts → Watch · Mkono. Writes only its OWN classes; no write to any governance class.
- **C9-034**: Attestation · watchdog · break-in test → the witness (neither authority's credentials; alarms outward).
- **C9-035**: Console sessions → the console's own authority. Trust Center under governance; Watch Console under Watch. Each session receipted under its own identity.

## Part IV — Handoff Contracts (§6)

Every crossing between services or authorities is a REGISTERED contract. An unregistered handoff is a finding.

- **C9-036 (§6 Admission → extraction)**: The frozen artefact and declaration cross whole. Doc 3 §5–§8 and Doc 4 §34.2 govern. Receipt = the admit row.
- **C9-037 (§6 Chokepoint → model)**: Custody chain per Doc 3 §13.1 with the input seat's verdict PRECEDING invocation. A hold renders through the refusal taxonomy. Receipt = trust receipt PLUS the verdict.
- **C9-038 (§6 Verdict → response)**: The seam of Doc 8 §6 — verdict row in, action out, policy version cited. NO OTHER CHANNEL between the engines exists.
- **C9-039 (§6 Containment → decision)**: A reserved-decision item crosses to the Decisions panel with its walk attached. Resolution crosses back as a ceremony-grade entry. Expiry governs the interim.
- **C9-040 (§6 Mirage release → reconciliation)**: Release verdict crosses to the replay-or-notify duty under the ruled posture. Discharge is receipted. The friction budget is debited.
- **C9-041 (§6 Disagreement → resolution)**: Both authorities' records cross side by side. The human's resolution lands in both readings as ONE entry. NEITHER record is amended — corrected by NEW ENTRY ONLY.
- **C9-042 (§6 Watch findings → CIF)**: Findings cross as detection input under CIF's own origination port. NOTHING crosses back but ratified frames through the coach seat.

## Part IV — Change & Precedence (§7)

- **C9-043 (§7)**: This charter changes by the SAME CEREMONY as any consequential rule — proposed, counter-signed, waited, applied, re-verified.
- **C9-044 (§7)**: Matrix changes carry the SLOWEST countdown class, because the matrix is the boundary of both authorities at once.
- **C9-045 (§7)**: Where this charter and any other document appear to disagree about WHO WRITES, WHO READS, or WHO IS ACTING, THIS charter governs.
- **C9-046 (§7)**: Where this charter is silent, the owning authority's canon governs.
- **C9-047 (§7)**: A silence that mattered is a finding to CLOSE BY AMENDMENT, never a gap to fill by recollection.

## Part IV — What it comes down to (§8)

- **C9-048 (§8)**: ONE account per record class, ONE identity per task, ONE contract per crossing — held in one short document both authorities cite.
- **C9-049 (§8)**: Seamlessness comes from the READS; boundaries come from the WRITES.
- **C9-050 (§8)**: The whole point of writing it down is that nobody, human or machine, EVER answers a matrix question from memory.
